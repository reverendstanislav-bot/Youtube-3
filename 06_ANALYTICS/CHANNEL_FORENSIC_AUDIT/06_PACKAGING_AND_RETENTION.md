## Title rubric

Five criteria,0-2 each. This is a bounded editorial checklist, not a CTR forecast or competitor benchmark. Any accuracy score of0 excludes a candidate regardless of total.

|Criterion|2points|1point|0points|
|---|---|---|---|
|C: clarity|Named company and understandable action|One needs context|Both unclear|
|S: stakes|Concrete financial outcome or specific consequential reversal|Generic conflict/lawsuit|Abstract idea|
|Q: question|Explicit contradiction or hidden mechanism the film explains|Generic why/how or chronology|No open question|
|M: brevity|<=65characters|66-80|>80|
|A: accuracy and opening|Accurate procedure/amount; promise appears within60s|Needs important qualifier or later payoff|Unsupported or misleading|

The length threshold is this audit's consistent rule, not a platform truncation guarantee. Equal totals are deliberately not force-ranked. Cost assumption:1-2 editor-hours for three title concepts plus ledger check;0 paid credits.

## Experiment controls and decision rules

1. Preserve existing nativeA/B tests; log exact variants and start dates. Do not restart because data is small. Test a new concept after the current window resolves, with title fixed for a thumbnail experiment.
2. Treat1000 eligible impressions per candidate as a feasibility checkpoint, not a power guarantee. At0.5%CTR that is about5 clicks, still highly noisy. Use native confidence/watch-time results or report INCONCLUSIVE. No promise of significance within14days.
3. Future opening test: one change family on the next comparable production. Primary metric first30/60s retention; guardrails AVD/APV,traffic mix and legal accuracy. Below100 views, qualitative direction only;100 is not automatically enough for causal proof. Report denominators and uncertainty.
4. Different topics, dates and runtime are observational comparisons. No native opening/runtime randomization was established here. Match age and sources where possible and call the result sequential learning.
5. Confirm end-screen elements and element impressions before testingCTA. End-screen click rate uses element impressions, not total video views. Missing denominator means effectiveness UNKNOWN.
6. Success: meaningful native result with preserved promise and qualified watch time, or valid new evidence that resolves the next decision. Failure: no valid observations, source-mix change, factual overpromise, or worse guardrails. INCONCLUSIVE is acceptable.
