# Publish & Analytics Schema

## Stage 17 Packaging
Store:
- title candidates;
- thumbnail concepts;
- promise;
- evidence/accuracy check;
- selected launch package;
- rejected concepts and reason.

## Stage 18 Upload
Store:
- canonical master URI/checksum;
- captions;
- description;
- pinned comment;
- playlist/end-screen plan;
- audience/category/disclosure settings;
- copyright/platform check result;
- publication/schedule time and URL.

## Stage 19 Analytics
Snapshots should record:
- timestamp since publication;
- impressions;
- traffic sources;
- CTR by source when available;
- views;
- watch time;
- AVD;
- APV;
- 30-second retention;
- top moments/dips;
- subscribers;
- comments/qualitative confusion;
- end-screen/next-view behavior when available.

Experiments record hypothesis, changed element, start/end, pre/post data and interpretation.
