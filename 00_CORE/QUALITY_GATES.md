# Quality Gates

## G00 Topic Qualification
PASS: recognizability/novelty, money stakes, conflict, hidden mechanism, consequence, sourceability and visual potential are sufficient; risk is manageable.

## G01 Evidence Research
PASS: core chronology, parties, money, disputed positions, primary evidence and current status are sufficiently documented to write without invention.

## G02 Case / Story Map
PASS: chronology is coherent; legal mechanism is understandable; story has escalation, reveals and consequence without distorting procedure.

## G03 Script V1
PASS: complete 15–22 minute target narrative (unless justified otherwise), click promise delivered, no unsupported material claim.

## G04 Fact + Legal Review
PASS: every material claim maps to ledger/source; wording matches status; no unresolved BLOCKER/HIGH accuracy issues.

## G05 Script Revision
PASS: accepted accuracy/narrative fixes applied without losing source traceability.

## G06 Performance Review
PASS: >=82/100 per Foundation metrics and no accuracy hard failure.

## G07 Script Lock
PASS: canonical script locked; claims ledger script refs updated.

## G08–G10 Voice/Audio
PASS: semantic fidelity, names/numbers/pronunciation, consistent channel voice, clean audio.

## G11 Timeline
PASS: complete runtime coverage and usable scene boundaries.

## G12 Visual Plan
PASS: every scene has a provenance-aware plan: authentic source/document, licensed/usable media, editor graphic, or clearly illustrative generation.

## G13 Visual Assets
PASS: assets are traceable, usable and do not misrepresent recreations as authentic evidence.

## G14 Edit Plan
PASS: document callouts, graphics, music/SFX and overlays are mapped to scenes/times.

## G15 Assembly
PASS: edit fulfills script and packaging promise; no material visual contradiction.

## G16 Final Fact/Legal Refresh
PASS: case status and time-sensitive facts refreshed after edit; title/script/graphics updated if needed; rights/provenance audit passes.

## G17 Packaging
PASS: title/thumbnail are accurate, strong and no more accusatory than evidence supports.

## G18 Prepublication
PASS: master, metadata, captions, sources/provenance and platform settings are ready.

## G19 Analytics
Ongoing; snapshots and experiments logged without rewriting history.
