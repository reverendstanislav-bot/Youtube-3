# Topic Qualification Schema

Use after initial discovery and before creating a durable VIDEO package.

Score each 0–5.

## Weighted score — 100
- Recognizability / extraordinary-story strength — 12
- Conflict clarity — 12
- Hidden mechanism / reveal — 12
- Primary-source availability — 12
- Story / escalation potential — 12
- Money / ownership / business stakes — 10
- Consequence / payoff — 10
- Competition gap / differentiation — 8
- Visual potential — 7
- Timeliness / current relevance — 5

Convert each 0–5 score proportionally to its weight.

## Hard gates
Regardless of total score:
- Sourceability must be >=4/5 for material disputed claims.
- Hidden mechanism must be >=3/5.
- Story/escalation must be >=3/5.
- Packaging must be possible without materially overstating legal status.
- Topic must not be generic doctrine-first content.

## Decision
- GO: >=78/100 and all hard gates pass.
- HOLD: 68–77, or strong concept with a resolvable blocker.
- NO-GO: <68 or any non-resolvable hard-gate failure.

## Qualification output
Record:
- score by dimension;
- hard-gate result;
- strongest hook;
- strongest primary-source path;
- competitor gap;
- legal/reputational risk;
- recommended working title family;
- GO / HOLD / NO-GO;
- reason.
