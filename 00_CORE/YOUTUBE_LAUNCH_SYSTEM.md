# YouTube Launch System

Primary target: Home / Browse / Suggested, not search-first growth.

## Three layers
1. APPEAL — title/thumbnail/topic selection.
2. ENGAGEMENT — opening, retention, conflict escalation, payoffs.
3. SATISFACTION — complete payoff, trust, next-view behavior.

## Packaging
Prepare at least three genuinely different concepts for a release-ready episode. Title and thumbnail complement rather than merely duplicate each other.

## Legal-story constraint
Curiosity may be aggressive; factual accusation may not be. Packaging cannot transform an allegation into a finding or imply a verdict that did not occur.

## Launch review
Observe impressions, traffic source, CTR, first-30-second retention, AVD/APV, top moments/dips, comments/confusion, subscribers and next-video behavior.

Do not panic-change packaging on tiny samples. Experiments are recorded in the episode analytics log.
