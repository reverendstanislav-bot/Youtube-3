# Preparation Block

The preparation block is the canonical pre-edit production system for every episode.

## Scope

Preparation covers Stage 00 through Stage 14.

- Stage 00 — Topic Qualification
- Stage 01 — Evidence Research
- Stage 02 — Case / Story Map
- Stage 03 — Script V1
- Stage 04 — Fact + Legal Review
- Stage 05 — Script Revision
- Stage 06 — YouTube Performance Review
- Stage 07 — Final Script Lock
- Stage 08 — Voice Script
- Stage 09 — Voice QA + Lock
- Stage 10 — Audio Master
- Stage 11 — Transcript + Visual Timeline
- Stage 12 — Visual Source / Generation Plan
- Stage 13 — Visual Asset QC
- Stage 14 — Graphics / Documents / Music / SFX Plan

Stage 15 Assembly/Edit begins only after preparation is complete.

## PREP_COMPLETE exit conditions

A video may become `PREP_COMPLETE` only when all applicable Stage 00–14 gates pass and:

- topic qualification is locked;
- evidence research is sourceable and current enough to write;
- the case/story map separates chronology from narrative order;
- the canonical final script is locked;
- all material factual/legal statements map through CLAIMS_LEDGER to SOURCE_INDEX;
- no unresolved BLOCKER/HIGH accuracy issue remains;
- voice input and final narration are locked;
- final audio exists and has passed QA;
- transcript/timeline cover the full runtime;
- every planned visual has a provenance class;
- authentic source visuals are distinguishable from recreations/illustrations;
- source visuals and generated assets have passed QC;
- graphics/document/music/SFX plans are complete enough for assembly;
- manifest, STATE, ledgers and asset records agree.

## Hard stop

Do not start Stage 15 merely because some assets exist.

The preparation block fails if the edit would require the editor to invent:
- factual meaning;
- legal status;
- missing chronology;
- unsupported visuals;
- unverified quotes/numbers;
- document authenticity;
- unresolved story structure.

## After preparation

- Stage 15 — Assembly / Edit
- Stage 16 — Final Fact + Legal Refresh + Copyright/Provenance Audit
- Stage 17 — Packaging
- Stage 18 — Upload / Prepublication
- Stage 19 — Post-Publish Analytics

Stage 16 deliberately occurs after edit because active legal matters can change during production.
