# Visual Bible — WHAT IT COST

Status: **ACTIVE**

Canonical identity rules live in:
`00_FOUNDATION/VISUAL_LOCK.md`

This file governs how that identity behaves inside episodes.

## Direction
Premium faceless business-documentary presentation mixing:
- authentic documents/screenshots when provenance and rights allow;
- editor-built timelines, money flows, ownership maps and contract callouts;
- product/company/location context;
- restrained illustrative reconstructions;
- generated imagery only when it cannot be mistaken for authentic evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy on screen
1. Real source document/evidence.
2. Editor-built representation derived from verified facts.
3. Clearly illustrative reconstruction.

Never fabricate a court filing, contract, email, signature or quote and present it as real.

Critical legal text, amounts, dates and quotes are editor-built from verified source text.

## Channel visual grammar
Use the locked WHAT IT COST system:
- Deep Black / Charcoal / Paper White base;
- Signal Red for selective consequence/evidence emphasis;
- condensed uppercase display typography;
- Inter for diagrams and supporting text;
- editorial serif only as restrained contrast;
- documents, redactions, amounts, ownership maps and timelines as recurring language.

## Motion / edit behavior
The static identity should carry into motion without becoming a motion-graphics template.

Prefer:
- deliberate push-ins;
- document reveals;
- clean highlights;
- timeline movement;
- restrained parallax;
- purposeful zoom/crop into evidence.

Avoid:
- constant glitch effects;
- fake CCTV overlays;
- overused typewriter gimmicks;
- random HUD graphics;
- decorative stock-law imagery;
- effects that obscure whether material is authentic.

## Reuse rule
Episode-specific visual tactics may evolve.

Do not promote a one-off technique into the channel-wide identity unless it proves reusable without reducing factual clarity or making every episode look identical.
