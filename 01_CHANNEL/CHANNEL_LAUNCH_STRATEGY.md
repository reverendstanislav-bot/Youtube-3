# Channel Launch Strategy

Status: channel-level operating direction; exact publication dates remain unlocked.

## First cluster goal
Train one coherent viewer expectation:
**recognizable business + hidden conflict + money + legal mechanism + consequence**

Initial shortlist:
1. PSA grading lawsuit
2. NautiStyles vs Bering Yachts
3. Suno / AI copyright
4. Steam / Valve legal battle
5. Waymo vs Uber
Reserve: UMG / DistroKid / AI music copyright

## Cluster logic
The first videos may vary by industry, but the viewer experience must remain consistent:
- recognizable entity;
- hidden business/legal conflict;
- strong stakes;
- document/evidence-driven reveals;
- plain-English mechanism;
- concrete consequence.

## Avoid at launch
Do not lead with highly saturated evergreen stories already heavily covered unless a genuinely new development or source angle exists.

Current examples to avoid as first episodes:
- Theranos
- WeWork
- Pepsi Jet
- McDonald's Hot Coffee
- Taylor Swift vs Scooter Braun
- Bricks & Minifigs

## Publishing principle
Quality and connected viewer expectation are more important than arbitrary cadence. Build adjacency between videos through related themes, playlists, end screens and pinned/description links once multiple videos are public.
