# Legal Editorial Policy

The channel reports and explains business/legal stories; it does not adjudicate them.

## Attribution
Use status-accurate language: “the complaint alleges”, “the company says/denies”, “prosecutors charged”, “the court held”, “the jury found”, “the parties settled”.

## Risk discipline
- prefer primary records and high-quality reporting for serious allegations;
- state current procedural status;
- include material denials/responses when relevant;
- do not assert motive unless documented or clearly framed as analysis;
- do not imply criminal conduct from civil allegations;
- avoid loaded labels unsupported by adjudicated facts.

## Civil vs criminal
Never blur civil claims, regulatory allegations and criminal charges/convictions.

## Settlements/dismissals/appeals
Report scope. Settlement does not prove liability. Dismissal of a count is not necessarily dismissal of a case. Appeals can change finality.

## Active matters
Final script and packaging must be refreshed immediately before release.
