# Claims Wording Guide

This guide translates evidence status into narration and packaging language.

## ALLEGATION / COMPLAINT
Safe pattern:
- “The complaint alleges…”
- “According to the lawsuit…”
- “The plaintiff says…”

Avoid:
- “X did…”
- “X stole/defrauded…” unless independently established by a stronger record.

## CLAIM / PARTY POSITION
Safe:
- “X argues…”
- “X says…”
- “X claims…”

## CHARGE
Safe:
- “Prosecutors charged X with…”
- “X was charged with…”

Avoid:
- “X committed…” before conviction/admission.

## RULING
Safe:
- “The judge ruled that…”
- “The court held…”
State the scope.

## VERDICT
Safe:
- “The jury found…”
State which claim/count and whether later proceedings matter.

## SETTLEMENT
Safe:
- “The parties settled…”
- “X agreed to pay…” when documented.

Avoid:
- “X admitted wrongdoing” unless expressly supported.

## DISMISSAL
Safe:
- “The judge dismissed [claim/case]…”
Add with/without prejudice or procedural basis when material.

Avoid:
- “The court proved the allegations were false.”

## CONVICTION
Safe:
- “X was convicted of…”
Record whether by plea or verdict and current appeal/post-trial status when material.

## DENIAL
Safe:
- “X denied the allegation.”
A denial is not proof of falsity.

## Titles / thumbnails
Packaging may compress wording, but it may not cross the underlying status boundary. If the punchiest verb requires a stronger status than the record supports, choose another verb.
