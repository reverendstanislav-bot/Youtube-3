# Fact Check Policy

## Core rule
Every material factual, numerical or legal assertion maps to `CLAIMS_LEDGER.csv`, which maps to `SOURCE_INDEX.csv`.

## Mandatory separation
Distinguish:
- party allegations;
- denials/responses;
- documentary evidence;
- court rulings;
- jury findings;
- settlements;
- responsible reporting;
- our analysis/inference.

## Quotes
Verify material quotes against the original source and context.

## Numbers
Record what a number represents and its date basis. A damages demand is not an award; a valuation is not cash paid; alleged losses are not adjudicated losses.

## Refresh
Run a fresh case/status check at Stage 16 before packaging/release.

## Uncertainty
If a material fact cannot be resolved, preserve uncertainty in the script rather than filling the gap with inference.
