# Competitor Research Policy

Competitor research is used to find **demand, saturation, packaging patterns and gaps** — not to copy scripts or recreate another video's treatment.

## Capture
For materially relevant competing/adjacent videos record:
- title;
- channel;
- URL;
- publish date;
- capture date;
- views at capture when available;
- approximate channel scale when useful;
- thumbnail pattern;
- hook;
- story angle;
- structure;
- what evidence/mechanism it emphasizes;
- what it omits;
- our differentiation opportunity.

## Compare at three levels
1. **Topic collision** — same case/company/event.
2. **Mechanic collision** — same hook structure such as founder lost company / contract backfired.
3. **Audience collision** — different story serving the same viewer appetite.

## Gap test
A viable gap can come from:
- newer legal development;
- better primary-source access;
- unexplained contract/ownership mechanism;
- overlooked money consequence;
- stronger chronology;
- a creator/company angle competitors missed;
- clearer visual explanation;
- better packaging that remains accurate.

## Do not
- treat competitor views as proof a topic will work for us;
- copy distinctive wording, narrative sequence or thumbnail composition;
- use another YouTube video as sole evidence for a factual/legal claim;
- call a topic “untapped” without actually checking direct collisions.
