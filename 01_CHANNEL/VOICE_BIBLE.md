# Voice Bible

Channel voice identity is governed by `00_FOUNDATION/VOICE_LOCK.md`.

## Delivery target
- controlled documentary narration;
- intelligent but accessible;
- curious rather than prosecutorial;
- strong reveal beats without tabloid shouting;
- precise names, dates, legal terms and dollar amounts;
- enough pause space for documents/graphics to land.

## Attribution
Vary phrasing naturally while preserving status: “the complaint says…”, “according to the filing…”, “the company denied…”, “the judge later ruled…”

Do not present the narrator as a lawyer, insider, investigator, witness or named real expert unless genuinely true and explicitly part of production.


## Spend control

Voice quality evaluation starts with existing provider previews and metadata.

New generated test audio is a paid-production action and requires explicit owner approval before submission, including agreed candidate count and estimated cost.

Never spend generation credits merely to narrow candidates when existing previews can be used first.
