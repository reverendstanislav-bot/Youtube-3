# Direct competitor research gate — evidence-first, four languages
Scope: WIC niche-screening and owner-requested reference-channel comparisons. Supplements COMPETITOR_RESEARCH_POLICY.md. This must not broaden a reference's niche based on generic business categories.

## A. Determine actual reference micro-niche BEFORE candidate counts
Minimum: reference channel URL + canonical channel ID/handle, date, **actual titles and links to at least 5 representative recent videos** (or ALL if <5), content language, repeatable subject/angle, length range, faceless vs host-led, and storytelling pattern. Record what is unknown.
When titles/video samples cannot be retrieved: REFERENCE_UNVERIFIED / HOLD; do not assign direct competitors or claim a market has <=3.

## B. Strict direct-competitor predicate
A channel counts DIRECT only with documented evidence meeting ALL:
1. Same micro-subject or story type (not merely Business/Education/History);
2. At least 3 comparable published videos with working links (or every video for <3-video channel);
3. Similar viewer promise / narrative mechanism and enough overlap to compete for the same watch session;
4. Comparable long-form editorial format, with any meaningful host/faceless difference disclosed;
5. Materially active within study window.
Mark ADJACENT otherwise and EXCLUDE from direct count. Unknown = UNVERIFIED, not zero. Do not count channels simply because recommender tools say "similar".

## C. Four separate language markets
US English (note international English spillover), Spanish-language (Spain vs Latin America distinguished), German-language, French-language. Country of channel registration is NOT audience geography. Search and record native-language synonyms, each market's query strings, search method and capture timestamp. Do not translate a channel's English audience into US-only assumptions.

## D. Reproducible evidence table — REQUIRED for every DIRECT candidate
`language | channel | channel_url | channel_id | market_assumption | sample_video_url_1..3 | sample_dates | sample_views_at_capture | long_form_duration | overlapping_micro_niche | matched_story_mechanism | format_match | verdict_DIRECT/ADJACENT/EXCLUDE/UNVERIFIED | evidence | capture_date`
Additionally record channel age, last upload, estimated momentum and source-of-metric if available. Compare equal windows and never infer viewer retention, CTR, revenue or RPM from public views.

## E. Saturation decision gate
- PASS <=3 ONLY when reference verified, systematic market-specific search logged, all candidates inspected, each DIRECT entry supported by sample videos, and a final check for overlooked small/emerging channels completed.
- HOLD if incomplete coverage, uncertain classification, missing original video titles, inaccessible platform, or unverified channel statistics.
- NO-GO / >3 only when at least 4 genuinely DIRECT candidates are verified; do not reject by counting broad adjacent channels.
- Use "confirmed minimum X" where discovery cannot prove exhaustive coverage; never use "exactly X" without documented search scope.
- Do not infer audience demand or future viral success from a single breakout channel.

## F. Output layout
1. Verified reference topic and 5 concrete examples;
2. search log per language and search coverage limitations;
3. separate DIRECT tables for four markets with VIDEO evidence, plus excluded candidates/reasons;
4. denominator/selection biases, recent small channel scan;
5. per-market status PASS/HOLD/NO-GO with rationale;
6. viable differentiated angles (not copied scripts).
Unknown does NOT mean absent. A data-catalog niche label or unattributed screenshot cannot replace video-level evidence.
