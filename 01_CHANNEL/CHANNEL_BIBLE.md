# Channel Bible

## Public brand
**WHAT IT COST**

Tagline:
**Where business decisions get expensive.**

Public promise:
**The hidden lawsuits, deals and decisions behind companies, creators and products people already know.**

Canonical brand authority:
`00_FOUNDATION/BRAND_LOCK.md`

## Positioning
Business/legal documentary storytelling for a US audience: hidden lawsuits, contracts, ownership fights and scandals behind recognizable companies, creators and products.

## Format
- English
- faceless
- long-form
- early target approximately 15–22 minutes
- story-first documentary structure
- law explained only as much as needed to understand the conflict and consequence

## Viewer promise
The viewer should leave understanding:
1. what looked normal on the surface;
2. what hidden conflict/mechanism existed underneath;
3. how money and incentives shaped behavior;
4. what the legal/contractual mechanism actually did;
5. what changed afterward;
6. what the decision, deal or conflict ultimately cost.

## Tone
Clear, skeptical, curious and cinematic without pretending disputed claims are proven.

Avoid:
- law-school jargon;
- moral grandstanding;
- tabloid certainty;
- law-firm visual or verbal clichés.

The brand should feel like a premium business documentary that happens to understand the legal mechanism, not a legal explainer channel.
