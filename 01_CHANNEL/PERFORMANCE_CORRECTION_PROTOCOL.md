# Performance correction protocol — WHAT IT COST

Effective 2026-10-08. Owner-authorized preparation after launch analysis.
Complements Foundation locks and Stages 06–19; episode analytics remain inside video packages.

## Evidence discipline
Separate measured outcomes, creative hypotheses and remedies. CTR cannot isolate thumbnail vs title vs topic vs audience. AVD cannot locate a dropout. Low end-screen CTR without exposure counts does not prove defective artwork. Missing related-video conversion data is unknown, not zero.
Technical/factual/creative PASS and actual audience performance are different verdicts. Never reuse an earlier draft's score to approve a rewrite. Never promise virality from projections or a small sample.

## Error register and acceptance
| Error | Correction | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Explanation before curiosity | Recognizable entity, concrete contradiction, stakes and a promised answer in the opening | Cold reviewer can state subject, stakes and question |
| Packaging exhausts the story | Reveal a compelling fact but preserve a meaningful mechanism/decision question | Title/thumbnail promise has a specific answer in the episode |
| Chronology replaces drama | Connect decisions to costs, incompatible goals and reversals | Each major sequence changes understanding or expectation |
| Empty rhetorical questions | Deliver evidence-backed answers before larger questions | Reveal map lists real payoffs, not question marks |
| Doctrine dominates the scene | Explain legal mechanism through its consequence; retain necessary attribution | No allegation upgraded to fact; no lecture without story purpose |
| Forced runtime | Remove repeated setup and conclusions | Every section adds evidence, consequence or causal understanding |
| Beautiful but abstract thumbnail | One identifiable subject and one clear stake/conflict | Phone-size inspection with title; no essential tiny text |
| Cosmetic A/B variants | Distinct supported propositions or visual theses | Separate test hypotheses; no invented winner |
| Repetitive static cards | Alternate proof, company/product context and purposeful process/money graphics | Normal-speed sequence review, not contact sheet alone |
| Decorative shake/crop jumps | Motivated cuts/reveals and smooth transitions | No random shake, continuous drift or abrupt reframing |
| Screen text competes with speech | One visual priority; highlight material source text | Mobile readability and narration alignment |
| Shorts are compressed lectures | One standalone tension, mini-payoff and a specific further question | Each contiguous extract independently reviewed; final duration <=30s including CTA |
| CTA after all curiosity closes | Point to a concrete further answer after an honest mini-payoff | CTA and related long-form promise agree |
| Volume scaled before feedback | Release strongest new Short first; inspect comparable response | Publication/measurement log; no unsupported suppression claim |
| Inflated internal PASS | Fresh independent current-artifact critique | Locations, evidence and acceptance criteria recorded |
| Repo behind publication | Record observed publication; preserve unknown platform settings | Manifest, STATE and index agree |

## Before voice production — Stage 06/07 recheck
Use exact canonical script, Shorts lock, claims/evidence and saved competitor research. Retain Foundation rubric: >=82/100 and all hard gates, with a fresh score for the actual version.
Required: first 0–5/5–15/15–30s map (estimated until audio); central question; decisions and reversals; actual reveal/payoff map; redundant/dense passages with exact section/paragraph; concrete ending and unresolved status; recognizable packaging brief; all Shorts reviewed in isolation.
Question count is not retention evidence. Preserve legal attribution in every extract. Canonical Shorts remain contiguous long-form audio; do not stitch/reorder/rewrite separate narration.
Owner approval remains historical fact. Pending corrective review blocks downstream generation without silently changing the script.

## Stages 12–15 — visual/edit review
Build rhythm around information and emotion. No fixed cut interval regardless of meaning. Alternate authentic close source crops, product/context images and editor graphics where useful.
Review actual first 60–90s, densest sequence, major reveals and ending at normal speed. Record timecodes for repetition, overload, slow/confusing passages, rough cuts and audio discontinuities. Contact sheets cannot prove pacing or sound.
Honor owner preferences: English narration/editorial text; no burned subtitles, shake or abrupt crop jumps; smooth intentional edit. Keep the 20s silent long-form end screen with correctly proportioned YouTube inserts. Shorts get their own <=30s total ending. Separate optional captions may be supplied when requested.

## Stage 19 — measurement
Capture T+24h/48h/72h/7d when they occur; do not invent earlier snapshots retrospectively. Record capture precision and report cutoff. Blank means unknown/unavailable; zero means explicitly measured zero. Keep rounded UI values approximate.
Longs: views, eligible impressions, overall/source CTR, watch time, AVD/APV, 30s retention, dips, subscribers, interactions, unique viewers, traffic and next-view outcomes where available.
Shorts: views, engaged views, feed exposures when exposed, stayed/swiped, AVD/APV, retention, interactions, subscribers, traffic and related-video outcomes when exposed. Engaged/views is not stayed-to-watch. Shorts thumbnail CTR is not feed swipe performance. Never use mixed-content CTR as long-form packaging CTR.
Audience: device/geography denominators, CC usage, new/returning, subscriber watch time, overlap where exposed. Mark suppressed/uncaptured metrics, including shares, dislikes, cards, playback/OS and source CTR. Revenue/RPM/CPM unavailable before monetization.
Working targets, not platform standards: CTR 3–4% after about 500 comparable eligible impressions; first30 retention 60%+; long APV 35%+; <=30s Shorts stayed 50%+ and APV 80%+. Consider source mix, sample size and trend. Targets cannot certify a prepublication PASS.
Log intervention hypothesis, changed element, pre/post windows and traffic. Simultaneous title/thumbnail changes test a package. Do not reset A/B after a handful of impressions.

## Cadence
Owner's every-two-day long schedule remains. Prepare earlier; cadence does not waive existing gates. No impulsive deletion/reupload. Suggested first new Shorts pacing: strongest <=30s candidate, then about one/day pending response; this is a feedback plan, not an algorithm rule.
