# Title System

## Goal
Recognizable subject + hidden conflict + stakes/consequence.

## Useful mechanics
- Just Got Caught
- It Backfired
- One Mistake Cost Millions
- One Deal Destroyed...
- The Real Contract Behind...
- Who Actually Owns...
- The Hidden Problem With...
- How a Founder Lost His Own Company
- The Billion-Dollar Fight Over...
- Company vs Creator
- Company vs Company
- One Tiny Clause Changed Everything

These are structures, not permission to overstate facts.

## Hard gates
- legal status must support the verb;
- money must be characterized correctly: demand, alleged damages, award, settlement, valuation, estimate, etc.;
- no title may imply conviction, liability, fraud or theft beyond evidence;
- active-case titles must survive Stage 16 refresh.

Generate multiple title families, not synonym swaps.
