# Document Visual Policy

## Classes
- AUTHENTIC_SOURCE — real filing/contract/email/statement/screenshot with provenance.
- EDITOR_RECREATION — editor-built layout derived from verified source content.
- ILLUSTRATIVE_RECONSTRUCTION — visual metaphor/reconstruction not claimed as original evidence.

## Rules
- Preserve source_id for authentic documents.
- Never alter a quote in a way that changes meaning.
- Crops/highlights may improve readability but must not hide material context.
- Recreated layouts must not be passed off as scans/originals.
- Generated imagery may not fabricate signatures, docket stamps, letterheads or quotes and imply authenticity.
- Exact critical text is editor-built from verified source content.
