# VIDEO 004 — Brief

## Working title
**Steam Tried to Stop Thousands of Claims. Then This Happened.**

## Subject / entities
Valve Corporation, Steam users pursuing arbitration, claimant law firms, the American Arbitration Association and courts addressing the resulting proceedings.

## Pillar
BUSINESS_LAWSUIT

## Core viewer promise
Explain how Steam's individual-arbitration system became the route for mass filings, why Valve rewrote the dispute rules, what Valve then asked courts to stop, and what the courts have and have not decided.

## Hidden mechanism
Class-action waiver + individual arbitration + fee allocation + mass filing scale → contract revision → attempt to redirect pending disputes into court → injunction and appeal fight.

## Stakes
Potentially substantial arbitration administration costs, thousands of consumer claims, Steam's platform rules and the broader business consequences of drafting dispute systems that behave differently at scale.

## Conflict
Steam users and their counsel pursued antitrust claims through the arbitration system Valve had required. Valve disputes the claims and challenged whether pending arbitrations could continue after the agreement changed.

## Consequence
The dispute already contributed to Valve eliminating mandatory arbitration and the class-action waiver from the Steam Subscriber Agreement. The status of pending proceedings remains contested.

## Target runtime
15–22 minutes.

## Packaging hypotheses
- Steam Tried to Stop Thousands of Claims. Then This Happened.
- Steam's Arbitration Clause Backfired
- The Contract Clause That Put Valve Against Thousands of Users

## Stage 01 research priorities
- Acquire and index the operative complaints, orders, appeal dockets and Steam agreement versions.
- Reconstruct claimant counts and changes without combining distinct cohorts.
- Trace every money figure to invoices, declarations, awards or pleadings.
- Record Valve's defenses and claimants' responses fairly.
- Map individual awards and vacatur petitions separately from the main injunction case.
- Audit direct YouTube competition and identify a non-derivative structure.
