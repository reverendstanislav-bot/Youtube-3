## HIGH blocker: Q053 / A00-P01 and standalone S01 — misleading implied connection between the 14,000 files and $179m award
**Current opening:** `Anthony Levandowski copied roughly 14,000 files from Google's self-driving project. In 2020, a court confirmed a personal civil judgment against him for more than $179 million.`
Each **isolated event is true**. The dangerous effect comes from placing the two sentences together with **no causal boundary**: especially in S01 cut from the film, a viewer is likely to read the giant judgment as punishment imposed for the 14,000 SVN-file downloads.
**Actual record:** S010 DOJ confirms Dec11 2015 ~14k SVN files copied to Google laptop, then Dec14 personal laptop. But **2020 $179,047,998.64 Google Judgment** came from separate Google Arbitration involving alleged breach of fiduciary duties/loyalty and contracts, and violation of California Unfair Competition Law; it is not a criminal judgment for stealing each SVN file, not Waymo's lawsuit vs Uber, nor a fine against Uber. Bankruptcy opinion S032 pp5–6 expressly describes this proceeding. One-count criminal plea concerned a particular Google Drive tracking file, not conviction over all 14k SVN files.
**Required:** add minimal in-paragraph distinction, e.g. *"A separate Google employment-related arbitration later produced a personal civil judgment of more than $179 million."* Preserve fast Anthony + 14k hook and the "not against Uber" payoff. Modify A00-P01 itself so S01 remains verbatim in-film. New isolated G04 is obligatory.
**Severity HIGH** because it makes the headline's foremost personal financial consequence sound causally tied to a different legal act. **No other paragraph inherits a true criminal-liability verdict from the settlement.**
