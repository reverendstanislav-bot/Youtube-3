## 1. The new story spine — ONE conflict

- 2018 Waymo civil matter → ~**$245m IN STOCK** corporate settlement (S005,S008,S022,S023).
- Separate Google v Levandowski arbitration → **$179,047,998.64 personal judgment**, not an Uber verdict or the federal criminal sentence (S032).

- 2022 Uber–Google–Levandowski global settlement → **Uber made a direct additional payment to Google by May 20, 2022, amount sealed**; Uber also undertook **$2m to the estate** to fund the plan (S032,S033). DO NOT say Uber paid the personal $179m in full.

**Four anchors viewers can remember:** copied files → purchase and risk terms → evidence nobody could conclusively explain → separate money judgments and actual payments. Save secondary corporate-history coda until AFTER this answer.

## 4. Three important delayed reveals (do not spoil in first minute)
1. **Actual corporate risk allocation** (A02→A03) not "Uber agreed to cover everything": conditions of indemnification were disputed later. The audience sees the clause **before** learning what it was invoked for.
2. **Evidence uncertainty** (A05): Stroz found no evidence of direct transfer; in court missing disks could support competing inferences. The order let jurors decide; no jury verdict followed. This is stronger than making up guilt.
3. **Actual cash obligation** (A08): 2018 *stock* settlement was not the last Google-related resolution involving Uber. A different **cash payment was made to Google by May 2022**, exact sum sealed, and a distinct $2m funded the plan. This completes opening question.
