## A07 — THE $179 MILLION PERSONAL JUDGMENT

**A07-P01**

Google had taken those claims into arbitration, a private process for resolving certain disputes. The allegations concerned Levandowski's employment duties and contractual obligations. They weren't a jury verdict against Uber. In March 2020, a court confirmed the arbitration award. This is where the other enormous number enters the story—and where the personal consequences become impossible to ignore.

**A07-P02**

On March 4, 2020, a California court confirmed a civil judgment against Anthony Levandowski personally: $179,047,998.64. Hours later, he filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection. This wasn't a fine imposed on Uber or a judgment from Waymo's lawsuit against the company. But it brought a disputed agreement from the Otto acquisition right back into the picture.
