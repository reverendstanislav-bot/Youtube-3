## Adversarial source-critical findings
1. **Copies versus transfer versus use:** The December 2015 approximately 14k internal-file copying is documented by DOJ; the English script does NOT claim the copies all entered Uber products. Source S010.
2. **Buyer diligence:** Delaware Supreme Court `McElrath v. Kalanick` describes preclose Stroz diligence and says the report found no evidence that the Google confidential material **Levandowski retained** went to Otto/third parties. English A03-P04 and S04 preserve that scope rather than universal exoneration. Source S008.
3. **Money from the Otto deal:** Court sources contrast $100k initial cash with potentially up to ~$680m consideration including conditional equity; English S03 does NOT say $680m was paid to the engineer. S008.
4. **Wrong-recipient email:** The alleged similarity of circuit-board drawings is not converted into a proved technology-use finding. S008/S003.
5. **Five disks:** March 2016 described disclosure, January 2018 permission for jurors to draw an adverse inference; NOT mandatory, contents NOT verified. English A05-P04 and S06 preserve this. S031.
6. **Waymo-Uber:** The February 2018 civil case ended in shares valued approximately $245m at the time, **not a cash criminal fine or a jury liability award**. S005/S008.
7. **Different proceedings:** $179,047,998.64 personal civil judgment March 4 2020, Chapter11 filed hours later; 33 original criminal charges yielded a **one-count guilty plea**, followed by 18-month sentence and Jan2021 pardon; do not collapse these into the Waymo corporate settlement. S032/S010/S011/S012.
8. **Legal payment basis (critical):** The 2025 bankruptcy opinion expressly says the Uber Main Payment was made under the **2022 Global Settlement**, **NOT pursuant to the 2016 Indemnification Agreement**. The court did not hold that disputed old indemnity agreement enforceable. English A08-P05, A09-P05 preserve the distinction. S032 PDF pp15/23.
9. **Sealed principal versus plan support:** The 2023 federal decision states the direct Uber-to-Google principal amount was **sealed** and separately references **$2m to the estate** for the Chapter11 plan; the 2025 opinion describes $2m to Levandowski for plan funding. The script uses only the common supported fact: $2m separately to fund the plan, without unverified bank-account-level payee wording. S033 PDF p3 and S032 PDF p6.
10. **2025 tax ruling:** Classified the Uber Main Payment as gross income to Levandowski; did **not** liquidate final tax liability or invalidate the settlement. S032 PDF p3.
