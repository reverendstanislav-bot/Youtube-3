# VIDEO 001 — Brief

## Working title
The eBay Harassment Scandal That Cost $56 Million

## Subject / entities
- eBay Inc.
- Ina and David Steiner / EcommerceBytes
- former eBay employees involved in the 2019 harassment/cyberstalking campaign
- former eBay executives named in the later civil litigation

## Pillar
CORPORATE_SCANDAL

## Core viewer promise
Show how frustration inside a globally recognizable company escalated into a bizarre harassment campaign, criminal consequences, corporate admissions, compliance costs and a 2026 civil settlement — tracing the business decisions and internal mechanisms that made the escalation possible.

## Hidden problem / mechanism
The episode is not primarily "people mailed cockroaches."

The hidden mechanism is how executive frustration, internal communications and a corporate security function turned criticism from a small ecommerce publication into an escalating retaliation problem, and how that ultimately became corporate legal and financial liability.

## Money / stakes
- eBay agreed to a $3 million criminal penalty in its 2024 deferred prosecution agreement.
- Reuters reported a 2026 civil settlement totaling approximately $55.7 million across eBay and three former executives, with eBay responsible for the largest share.
- Exact settlement allocation must be sourced precisely in Stage 01 before final packaging uses "$56 million."

## Conflict
A major public company and members of its organization versus two publishers whose reporting and reader comments frustrated eBay leadership.

## Consequence
- seven former employees pleaded guilty;
- individual prison/probation/fine consequences;
- eBay entered a deferred prosecution agreement, admitted a detailed factual recitation, paid a $3 million criminal penalty and accepted an independent compliance monitor;
- the civil litigation was resolved in July 2026.

## Why now
The July 28, 2026 civil resolution gives the story a fresh ending and makes the total corporate/personal cost clearer than earlier coverage.

## Target runtime
15–22 minutes.
