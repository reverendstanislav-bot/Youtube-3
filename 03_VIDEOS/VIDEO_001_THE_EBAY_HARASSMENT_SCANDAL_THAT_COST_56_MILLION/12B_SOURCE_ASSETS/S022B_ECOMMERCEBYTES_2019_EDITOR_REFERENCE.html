Thank you

After 27 years, EcommerceBytes has ceased publication.

Steiner Associates, LLC publishers David and Ina Steiner began an incredible journey when they launched AuctionBytes in 1999, rebranding the site to EcommerceBytes in 2011.

Freelance reporters, advertisers, and providers supported what remained a small, husband-and-wife publishing business dedicated to helping sellers become more efficient and profitable selling online.

Readers provided insight into the joys and pitfalls of selling online. Many people who relied on ecommerce platforms for their livelihood requested anonymity in communicating with EcommerceBytes about problems they encountered because they feared retaliation for speaking out publicly. The publishers of EcommerceBytes support reporter shield laws such as the one being considered in Massachusetts, supported by the Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press and the Massachusetts Newspaper Publishers Association.

According to the Legal Information Institute:

"In the context of the press, shield laws allow journalists to refuse to disclose confidential sources of information obtained during newsgathering. These laws protect reporters from being compelled to testify about their sources or unpublished materials in legal proceedings.

Thank you for supporting and being a part of EcommerceBytes over the past three decades.
