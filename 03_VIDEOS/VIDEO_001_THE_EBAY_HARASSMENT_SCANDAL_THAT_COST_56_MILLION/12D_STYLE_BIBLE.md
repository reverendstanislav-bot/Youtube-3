# Stage 12D — VIDEO_001 Production Style Bible

Status: **LOCKED — OWNER-APPROVED DIRECTION**

Video: **The eBay Harassment Scandal That Cost $56 Million**

This file turns the approved proof direction into a production contract. It extends `00_FOUNDATION/VISUAL_LOCK.md`; it does not replace the channel identity.

## 1. Core visual principle

**Premium investigative business documentary — evidence, money, power, consequence.**

The approved VIDEO_001 proof language is:
- dark charcoal/black documentary field;
- warm paper/evidence materials;
- real source imagery and real document crops as the factual core;
- restrained signal-red emphasis;
- condensed high-impact headlines;
- dossier / evidence-board / court-document composition;
- clean negative space;
- lower-frame subtitle reserve;
- serious editorial tension without horror or tabloid styling.

The final video must look **constructed from evidence**, not decorated with legal clichés.

## 2. Proof reference lock

The approved proof direction is represented by these archetypes:
1. **Authentic evidence treatment** — real DOJ/FBI evidence image treated as documentary evidence, not shock art.
2. **Executive legal-status dossier** — real/source-safe executive material + precise civil/criminal status.
3. **Court document highlight** — real court crop, one exact quote/line highlighted, source/date visible.
4. **Money / settlement breakdown** — one dominant total, clean allocation, explicit legal caveat.
5. **Geography / operation timeline** — verified map + date spine + real location context.
6. **Sentencing / participant board** — derived from the same dossier/paper system; exact DOJ/court-backed person-specific status.

**Important:** proof mockups are style references only. Generated/recreated proof imagery is never itself evidence. Production frames must use the real assets and source crops locked in Stage 12C.

## 3. Palette — HARD LOCK

Use the channel palette:
- Deep Black — `#0B0B0B`
- Charcoal — `#1F1F1F`
- Slate — `#3A3A3A`
- Paper White — `#EDEDED`
- Signal Red — `#D32222`
- Antique Gold — `#B69B68` — rare only

Paper/evidence substrates may visually warm Paper White toward beige through texture/lighting, but warm paper is a **material treatment**, not a new accent color.

### Red usage
Signal Red is semantic. Use it for:
- the operative word in a headline or quote;
- a key dollar figure;
- a date/event marker;
- a legal/status warning;
- one route or causal line;
- one evidence highlight.

Rules:
- normally one dominant red emphasis per frame;
- never flood the frame with red;
- no red horror glow, blood-splatter styling, neon red, or full-screen red wash;
- red must clarify hierarchy, not merely create intensity.

## 4. Typography — HARD LOCK

### Display
**Bebas Neue** or an approved condensed production substitute with the same geometry.

Use for:
- major headline;
- dominant amount;
- section/event title;
- short status labels.

1920×1080 reference:
- hero headline: approximately 92–150 px;
- dominant amount: approximately 130–210 px;
- secondary headline: approximately 58–88 px.

### Support
**Inter**.

Use for:
- body copy;
- dates;
- roles;
- legal status;
- source lines;
- labels;
- map/location text.

1920×1080 reference:
- body: approximately 34–48 px;
- support/status: approximately 28–38 px;
- source/legal note: approximately 20–28 px.

### Editorial serif
**EB Garamond** only as a restrained accent for an authentic/editorial quote treatment. Never use it as the UI or main headline family.

## 5. Headline / body / label rules

### Headline
- one dominant idea;
- normally 2–7 words;
- may break to 2–4 lines;
- must remain readable at phone size;
- use Signal Red on only the semantically important word/amount;
- never compete with captions.

### Body
- explain only what the viewer must understand at that beat;
- maximum 2 short blocks;
- avoid paragraph walls;
- do not repeat narration verbatim unless the exact wording itself is evidence.

### Labels
Allowed labels:
- COURT RECORD
- DOJ / FBI SOURCE IMAGE
- AUTHENTIC CASE EVIDENCE
- CIVIL CASE
- CRIMINAL CASE
- ATTEMPTED — NOT INSTALLED
- NOT A JUDGMENT
- SOURCE / DATE / ROLE / LOCATION

Labels must be factual, source-bound and short. Never invent police/evidence file numbers, dates, agency stamps, case metadata or chain-of-custody markings for atmosphere.

## 6. Paper textures / evidence frames

Paper is a material layer, not a scrapbook gimmick.

Allowed:
- subtle torn edge;
- photocopy / print texture;
- restrained fold / wear;
- paper shadow;
- one or two stacked source sheets;
- restrained tape/clip treatment where editorially useful.

Forbidden:
- fake blood;
- dirty horror stains;
- piles of arbitrary paper;
- random stamps that imply authenticity;
- fake signatures;
- invented docket numbers;
- fake evidence markers;
- unreadable decorative type.

Authentic source crops remain visibly authentic. Editor-created labels must be visually separable from the source document.

## 7. Subtitle-safe zone — HARD PRODUCTION RULE

### 16:9 master — 1920×1080
Reserve the **bottom 20% of every frame**:
- Y = **864–1080**
- height = **216 px**

Inside this band:
- no essential headline;
- no material dollar figure;
- no person’s eyes/face;
- no evidence object that must be understood;
- no exact quote;
- no source/date label;
- no legal caveat;
- no map endpoint;
- no important UI/diagram node.

Captions may occupy this reserved band.

Everything essential must still work if the entire bottom 216 px is temporarily covered.

### 9:16 Shorts derivative — 1080×1920
Reserve the **bottom 22%** for captions/platform UI:
- Y ≈ **1498–1920**

For beats included in Shorts, build an alternate 9:16 layout from the **same factual assets** when a simple crop would damage hierarchy. Do not rewrite narration or invent a different visual claim.

## 8. Composition rules

- keep the factual focal area primarily in the upper 78–80% of frame;
- one primary visual object + one evidence/context layer + one explanatory layer;
- negative space is intentional;
- source labels are secondary;
- legal caveats must remain readable but not overpower the main idea;
- avoid face → face → face or document → document → document repetition when the beat plan allows a location/map/evidence change;
- never cover faces with text cards;
- never let decorative art overlap a factual quote or amount.

## 9. Documentary truth hierarchy

Production priority:
1. authentic source / evidence;
2. licensed real-world context;
3. editor-native factual graphics;
4. illustrative reconstruction only if a later proof exposes a real gap.

Generated imagery:
- cannot impersonate authentic evidence;
- cannot create likenesses of real case participants for production;
- cannot fabricate a specific act as though photographed;
- must be labelled if realistic reconstruction is ever approved later.

## 10. Legal visual locks

Always preserve:
- allegation ≠ fact;
- charge ≠ conviction;
- surviving claim ≠ liability;
- DPA ≠ conviction;
- settlement ≠ judgment;
- $55.7M civil settlement package ≠ eBay-only payment;
- separate $3M criminal DPA penalty ≠ part of the $55.7M civil package;
- attempted GPS tracker ≠ successfully installed tracker;
- Wenig / Wymer / Jones were not criminally charged over the campaign.

No visual shorthand may override these distinctions.

## 11. Forbidden production patterns

**HARD FORBIDDEN**
- key content inside the lower subtitle-safe band;
- cheap crime-poster aesthetic;
- horror / gore treatment;
- fake CCTV look;
- glitch / HUD overload;
- giant black information cards floating over real footage;
- card-grid dashboards as the default visual language;
- overcrowded multi-panel frames;
- neon accents;
- generic gavel/scales/courthouse cliché as default;
- fabricated source documents;
- generated real-person likenesses;
- fake evidence labels/stamps;
- repeated identical layout three beats in a row;
- decorative graphics covering captions, faces, quotes or legal notes.

## 12. Motion implication for later assembly

These styleframes are designed for restrained editorial motion:
- slow push-in on authentic crop;
- masked document reveal;
- highlight sweep;
- date spine progression;
- map line reveal;
- amount/count-up only when factual;
- subtle parallax on real location/context images.

No shake, whip-zoom, aggressive bounce, glitch or constant kinetic typography.
