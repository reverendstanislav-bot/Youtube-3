## Global audio rules
- Voice master remains priority. Music supports pacing; it never supplies factual meaning.
- No horror sound design, jump scares, pig squeals, insect foley, police sirens, gunshots, cash-register sounds or “guilty” gavel clichés.
- No SFX may imply an event not established in the record.
- Exact quotes, legal qualifiers and money arithmetic get more space, not more noise.
- Keep VO roughly 6–10 dB above the music bed; add 3–5 dB ducking during dense legal/number passages.
- Music/SFX must be YouTube-safe. Final track files require source/license/author entries in the provenance manifest before master approval.
- Prefer one coherent music family with 3–4 intensity variants rather than many unrelated tracks.
- No CTA audio before or over the final WHAT IT COST payoff.
