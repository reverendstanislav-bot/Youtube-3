## Channel-wide non-monotony rule
Consistency comes from palette, typography, paper/evidence treatment and source-first hierarchy — not repeated identical layouts.

Every prompt must vary at least two of:
- source crop;
- focal side;
- paper layering;
- thematic cue;
- document/photo balance;
- map/timeline geometry;
- headline placement.

No unrelated source may be introduced merely to create variation.

## Subtitle rule
Every frame reserves at least bottom 20%, target 22%, as visually quiet caption space.
