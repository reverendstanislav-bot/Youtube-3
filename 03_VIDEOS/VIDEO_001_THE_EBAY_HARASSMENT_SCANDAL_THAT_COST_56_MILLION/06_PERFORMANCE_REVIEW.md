## 1. Hook — 11 / 12

The cold open is strong because it creates a fast contradiction:

**bizarre harassment objects -> eBay security organization**

It delivers recognizable brand, danger, consequence and money within the opening section.

Strengths:
- concrete objects;
- recognizable company;
- immediate "this was not random trolling" inversion;
- guilty pleas / DPA / $55.7M establish that the story has a real ending;
- central question is organizational, not merely criminal.

Why not 12/12:
The opening reveals several later consequences at once — guilty pleas, DPA and the settlement package.

That does not kill suspense because the real question is "how did this happen?", but delivery should avoid making the first 40 seconds feel like a summary.

Lock-time direction:
**read the consequence lines faster and visually tease rather than fully explain them.**

Do not remove the $55.7M promise.
