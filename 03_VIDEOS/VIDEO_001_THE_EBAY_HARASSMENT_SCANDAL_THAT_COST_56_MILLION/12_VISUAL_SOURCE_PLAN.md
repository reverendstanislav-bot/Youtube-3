## Allocation logic
1. HIGH legal beats default to authentic primary records plus editor-native typography.
2. Real people are archive/photo/name-card only; no generated likenesses.
3. Internal quotes are extracted from S009 and typeset exactly.
4. Money graphics are editor-native and retain separate legal categories.
5. Surveillance/tracker reconstructions are allowed only as labelled illustration and may never show a successful tracker installation.
6. Harassment-package reconstruction may be used when no licensable authentic visual exists, but never as fake evidence.
7. Maps are editor-built, factual and restrained.
8. Stage 12 does not acquire or QC final assets; acquisition/QC remains downstream.

## Publication-safe source rebuild — 2026-09-22

The Stage 12 source layer is being rebuilt with a strict separation between:
- factual/evidentiary authority;
- visual reuse rights;
- final asset approval.

New control artifacts:
- `12B_PUBLISHABLE_SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv`
- `12B_DOCUMENT_CROP_PLAN.csv`
- `12B_GAP_SOURCE_RESOLUTION.csv`
- `12B_RIGHTS_MATRIX.md`
- `12B_SOURCE_REBUILD.md`

Current rules:
1. S009 now uses the official GovInfo court PDF; the Justia copy is backup only.
2. Government/court document crops use exact official pages and preserve context.
3. DOJ source material may be reused only where DOJ/public-domain status applies; DOJ seals/logos are excluded unless separately authorized.
4. Third-party material embedded in government documents is not automatically treated as public domain.
5. Creative-Commons context assets must retain their exact license and attribution/share-alike obligations.
6. Reuters, Bloomberg, eBay-owned statements, EcommerceBytes pages and other copyrighted reporting/pages are not silently promoted to license-cleared visual assets.
7. Natural Earth public-domain data is the map base.
8. Literal reconstruction is not replaced by a contextually wrong stock image merely because that image is freely licensed.
9. Source alternatives may reduce later generation needs, but the six-image generation ceiling is not changed until proof/QC establishes which reconstructions are genuinely unnecessary.
