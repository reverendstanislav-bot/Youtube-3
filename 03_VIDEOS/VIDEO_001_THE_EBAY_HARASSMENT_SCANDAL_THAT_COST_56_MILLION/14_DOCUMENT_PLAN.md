## Core rule
Authentic filings/messages are evidence objects, not decoration. Never redraw legal body text with AI. Use a tight real crop, or editor-native recreation from verified wording where rights require it.

## Placement
- Source/date labels live in the upper 12% or upper-side margin; never inside the bottom 22% subtitle-safe zone.
- Default source label is visually secondary to the beat headline.
- Do not show a full dense page unless the narration specifically needs the page identity; otherwise crop to the exact verified passage.
- Highlight with one restrained deep-red underline/bracket only.
- No fake stamp, fake seal, fake signature, fake docket text or invented legal wording.
- Keep 1–2 context lines when a quote could change meaning without them.
- Never obscure a qualifier that changes the legal meaning.

## Authenticity classes
- **AUTHENTIC_CROP / AUTHENTIC_SOURCE_PAGE_CROP** — use the real rendered crop.
- **TIGHT_EDITOR_CROP** — crop the real PDF at edit time; no retyping of legal body copy.
- **EDITOR_RECREATION_FROM_PRIMARY / SOURCE** — typeset only verified wording because the literal page is reference-only or rights-sensitive.
