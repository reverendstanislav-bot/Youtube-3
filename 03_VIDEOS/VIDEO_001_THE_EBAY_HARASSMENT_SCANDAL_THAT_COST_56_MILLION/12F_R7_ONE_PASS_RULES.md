## Canonical visual language
WHAT IT COST canonical final-frame style: premium investigative business documentary; charcoal/black textured background; warm aged-paper evidence layers; off-white distressed condensed headline; restrained deep-red underline/tab accents; tiny brass/black office hardware only when useful; realistic paper depth and film-grain; strong cinematic negative space; serious corporate/legal tone; no horror, no sensational crime-poster language, no neon, no dashboard/card-grid, no decorative clutter.

## One-pass target
These prompts are written to minimize reject/edit risk. No model can guarantee a zero-reject rate, so the production standard is: no known prompt defect, no intentional cleanup dependency, no fake-document dependency, and no planned semantic post-fix.

## Hard rules
1. Bottom 22% is always subtitle-safe and visually quiet.
2. Generated readable text is limited to the locked headline + optional support line.
3. Authentic source crops are factual inputs and must not be semantically rewritten.
4. Legal/document crops are photographed source objects inside the composition, never AI-retyped documents.
5. No real-person likeness synthesis; attached real photos may be framed/cropped but not reinvented.
6. No random courthouse, police, GPS, money, insects, packages or redaction motifs unless the beat requires them.
7. No horror, gore treatment, cheap crime-poster look, dashboard/card grids, neon, fake UI, watermark or borders.
8. One hero idea + one support idea maximum; compositions vary by T01/T02/T03/T04/T06/T07 family to avoid monotony.
9. Legal status, chronology, attribution and dollar amounts are immutable.
10. Accepted frames are not regenerated.
