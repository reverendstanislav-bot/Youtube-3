## Final description

```text
In 2019, a campaign run by people working in or with eBay’s security organization targeted EcommerceBytes publishers Ina and David Steiner with disturbing deliveries, online harassment, surveillance, and an attempted GPS-tracker installation.

This documentary follows how criticism of eBay moved through internal messages and corporate security, how all seven federally prosecuted participants pleaded guilty, why eBay later entered a deferred prosecution agreement and paid a separate $3 million criminal penalty, and how the civil case ultimately ended in a $55.7 million settlement package in July 2026.

Important legal distinction: the $55.7 million figure was a civil settlement package — not a judgment and not an eBay-only payment. The separate $3 million DPA penalty is not included in that total. Claims that survived summary judgment were not final findings of liability, and the civil case settled without a jury verdict on those questions.
```
