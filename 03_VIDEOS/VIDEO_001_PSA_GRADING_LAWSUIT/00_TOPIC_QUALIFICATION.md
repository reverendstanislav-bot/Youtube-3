## Hard gates

### Sourceability — PASS
A federal complaint was filed July 28, 2026 in the District of Maryland, case 1:26-cv-02933. A public docket record exists, and the filed complaint is publicly mirrored. Primary-source acquisition is practical.

### Hidden mechanism — PASS
The story has a clear mechanism: a grading service whose number affects market value is challenged by allegations that grading reliability, criteria and related business incentives are not what customers understand them to be.

### Story / escalation — PASS
The filed complaint supplies a long historical arc, specific grading examples, business-model allegations, a class-action/RICO mechanism and multiple possible consequences. Related 2026 litigation creates context without needing to merge separate cases.

### Packaging accuracy — PASS WITH GUARDRAIL
The topic can be packaged strongly without asserting liability. The original "$3 Billion Lawsuit" phrasing is not locked because "$3B" is better understood as a possible treble-damages theory built on alleged losses, not a court-established amount.

### Doctrine-first risk — PASS
The viewer-facing story is about PSA, card values, grading trust and business incentives. RICO/class-action law is explanatory machinery, not the topic itself.
