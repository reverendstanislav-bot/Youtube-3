# Stage 01 — Evidence Research — Fyre Festival Financial Fraud

## Story spine / original angle
The festival became famous for an embarrassing experience, but federal records describe a larger method: fictional booking volume -> fictional revenue -> representations of financial strength and insurance -> investments and vendor payment -> festival collapse -> another deceptive VIP ticket business while awaiting sentencing.

Do not make the tent footage the story's main payoff; the larger discovery is that supposed commercial traction was manufactured.

## Verified principal facts
1. Actual artist-booking income was $57,443 over approximately May 2016–April 2017; doctored reports described millions (SRC001 SRC007).
2. Investors saw representations of >2,500 artist bookings in ONE MONTH; records showed 60 in ONE YEAR (SRC001). Never call these confirmed festival performances.
3. A doctored brokerage statement exaggerated purported stock holdings from under $1,500 to over $2.5m (SRC002 SRC003).
4. False claims of rights to event-cancellation insurance payouts were used to induce festival investments although no policies were executed (SRC001).
5. Original criminal case described >$24m investor loss plus $2m vendor loss; original forfeiture $26,040,099.48 (SRC001).
6. Separate SEC 2018 offerings across Fyre Media, Fyre Festival and Magnises: at least $27.4m raised; different scope, not an additive loss (SRC002).
7. After the Fyre scheme McFarland ran NYC VIP Access; July 26 2018 additional guilty pleas covered wire fraud, bank fraud and false statements (SRC006).
8. October 11 2018 sentence: six years in prison, final forfeiture $26,191,306.28 (SRC007).

## Exact-number accounting
$26m is shorthand for DOJ investor+vendor losses, not ticket refunds, not festival production costs and not SEC fundraising totals. DOJ criminal forfeiture final amount $26,191,306.28. SEC $27.4m offering proceeds covers multiple entities. SEC disgorgement agreement was to be satisfied against related criminal forfeiture; never double count.

## Legal status
McFarland guilty pleas = admitted and convicted conduct. June 2018 new charge is not itself proof; use July 26 guilty plea. Other SEC respondents' civil settlements were without admission/denial in 2018 SEC release. Do not assign McFarland's admissions to all company staff/influencers.
