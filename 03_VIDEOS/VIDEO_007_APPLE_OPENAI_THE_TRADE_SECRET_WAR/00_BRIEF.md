# VIDEO007 — Stage00 Owner-Approved Brief
Date: 2026-10-16. Channel: WHAT IT COST (US English). Pillar: AI hardware / trade secrets.

**Working title:** Apple Accused OpenAI of Stealing Secrets. Then Everything Reversed.
**Civil case:** Apple Inc. v. Liu et al, N.D. Cal. 5:26-cv-07078-EJD.

## Hook
Apple sues OpenAI accusing former employees of taking Apple hardware secrets. Then, in October, OpenAI alleges one of its own former staff transferred more than 100 confidential unreleased-device files to personal accounts and returned to Apple. Opposing claims, same hardware race — whose information went where? All are unproven allegations.

## Verified event spine
- **2026-07-10** Apple filed **ECF 1**, 41-page complaint against former employees / OpenAI / io, alleging trade secret misappropriation and contract breaches. **Not established facts**.
- **2026-10-13** judge issued **ECF 175**, granting only **part** of Apple's motion for expedited discovery (other requests denied or deferred). Procedural order, not liability finding.
- **2026-10-14** OpenAI submitted **ECF 176**, *Defendants' Supplement to Joint Case Management Statement*: alleged more than 100 sensitive files about unreleased OpenAI hardware were transferred to personal accounts by an employee who returned to Apple; it expressed concern about a presentation to Apple leadership and requested opportunity to investigate and **potentially** file counterclaims. **No counterclaim filed by ECF 176.**
- Apple disputes OpenAI's assertions and reports it found no evidence of OpenAI data; OpenAI denies Apple's allegations.

## WHAT IT COST angle
Real stakes: IP protection, AI device development, hiring competitive talent, discovery and possible future litigation. **No confirmed judgment, damages or dollar loss yet.** Never invent a '$XB cost' from a separate deal.
