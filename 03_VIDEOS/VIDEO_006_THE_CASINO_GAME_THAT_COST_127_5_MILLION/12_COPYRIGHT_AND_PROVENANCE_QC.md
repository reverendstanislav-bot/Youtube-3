# VIDEO006 — Rights-safe footage acquisition and documentary use protocol (Stage12 R2)

## License confidence classifications

1. `PEXELS_GENERAL_COMMERCIAL`: platform states commercial use and no platform attribution; **verify individual item and embedded third-party rights**, especially slot UI/game textures/logos and people. Protect from misleading source/evidentiary identification. Pexels source must remain from the actual item creator, with platform URL and downloaded original hash. Short editorial crop/grade/transitions are generally adaptations allowed by site license; cannot re-sell stock unchanged. No absolute guarantees against Content ID.
2. `CC_BY_3_0`: Wikimedia Commons individual source page has attribution condition. The Dronalist original Las Vegas Strip aerial 2021; credit author + original source + license link + modification note in video description, no implied association with the case. Additional protected company signs in the scene require editorial/nominative use check.
3. `MIXKIT_RESTRICTED`: DO NOT use free personal-use-only slot/poker videos in monetized WHAT IT COST. Premium Envato tier commercial rights may exist but would require purchase and approval; we excluded specific links.
4. `YOUTUBE_STANDARD`: not a license to download/re-upload; case-specific footage may be high value but is under copyright. Seek written permission or legal-reviewed transformative fair use only, with separate owner risk decision. Extracted official/company promotional screenshots are not automatically free just because the company posted them.

## Per-video mandatory asset ledger at Stage13

Record: unique source URL and creator; downloaded master file SHA256; native resolution/fps/duration; exact usable in/out ranges in source file; clip type/subject/year; original site's license version and capture date; platform permission; faces/trademarks/copyrighted slot-art risk and releases; any attribution-required credit to append to YouTube description; claim-linked scene ids and picture usage; file's footage QC verdict; no more than 2 repeated passages from same source unless editor approves documentary callback. Each master timeline placement in `12_VIDEO_FOOTAGE_USAGE_MAP.csv` is NOT a source trim timestamp.

## On-screen strict truth rules

- Stock slot reels never represent actual Dragon Train footage or source evidence. Genuine cabinet-product image = rights HOLD and source, not free reusable.
- Generic stock 'employees around contracts' are NOT real litigants, witnesses, historical events, or lawyers involved in this case. No speech from stock videos should be mixed into narration.
- The matter is a trade-secret **civil** case; no handcuffs, police raids, crime scenes or fabricated verdict.
- No newly generated fake filing, original Excel, code screen, money transfer receipt, historic expo video, actual Australian ruling or real judge scene.
- Include source attribution where required and avoid defamatory implied source identification.
