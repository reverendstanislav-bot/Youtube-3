## 1. ONE-PARAGRAPH FACTUAL ANSWER
Aristocrat, the developer of successful casino game families including Lightning Link (2014) and Dragon Link (2016), brought US and Australian trade-secret / IP litigation against Light & Wonder following development and launch of the rival Dragon Train game, later expanding allegations regarding Jewel of the Dragon. After a particularly strong **September 2024 federal preliminary injunction** against Dragon Train, followed by a **July 2025 stipulated expansion** to Jewel of the Dragon, both companies resolved their disputes by a Jan 11 2026 settlement. The joint statement and later signed federal court order confirm L&W acknowledged use of certain Aristocrat mathematical information in the two game families and undertook **permanent cessation** of commercialization and covered-info non-use/destruction. **USD $127.5 million was paid**, as stipulated to the court on Feb26 2026. US claims/counterclaims were dismissed and **both interim injunctions vacated**, while contractual obligations survived. A 2026 issuer SEC filing additionally says relevant Australian claims against specified L&W defendants were dismissed March11 2026. This was not a criminal case or full jury verdict imposing $127.5m damages. [S001,S005,S006,S007,S008]

## 2. Characters and corporate identities
**Plaintiff:** Aristocrat Technologies Inc. and Aristocrat Technologies Australia Pty Ltd; global Aristocrat Leisure is named in public joint announcement. The winning product in the US motion was Aristocrat's **Dragon Link**, with the earlier **Lightning Link** also relevant in court descriptions of proprietary models.

**Defendants:** Light & Wonder Inc, LNW Gaming Inc and SciPlay Corporation (US docket 2:24-cv-00382-GMN-MDC). L&W's predecessor corporate name Scientific Games is relevant when dating employees, not a separate allegation of liability.

**Named employee:** Emma Charles — per publicly available preliminary order, worked for Aristocrat 2008–July2017, joined Scientific Games (later L&W) July2021 as senior mathematician, worked on Dragon Train beginning late2021 and ultimately led mathematical direction. The Court considered her prior Dragon Link work and documentary evidence of shared worksheets at the **preliminary relief** stage. Employment chronology is not by itself a proven wrongful act. **L&W CEO's January2026 reference to 'a former employee' is not itself a judicial finding of personal guilt or an expressly named attribution to Charles.** Narration may name Charles when using the signed court decision and attribute interim findings, but must never make criminal or career-damaging unsourced embellishments. [S001,S002,S006]

**Other team members:** Wil Soo and Yuri Leontiev gave testimony described by the preliminary judge regarding independent math work. The opinion discusses their contribution and explains why the judge was not persuaded on key spreadsheet connections. Do not erase their testimony. [S001]

**Leadership statements:** Aristocrat CEO Trevor Croker emphasized the importance of IP and success of protective injunction; L&W CEO Matt Wilson publicly said a former employee used certain math without company knowledge and contrary to policy. These are **party explanations**, NOT jointly adjudicated findings about the entire company's knowledge. [S006]

## 3. Hidden mechanism — what “the math” means
These are electronic casino slot games with themed audiovisual presentation, spinning reels, progressive prizes and a Hold & Spin bonus. Underneath gameplay, confidential design decisions and calculations affect symbol frequencies, prize tables, jackpot probability, base-game structure and “math model” worksheets. Distinguish:
1. Public-facing rules, animation, symbols, general Hold & Spin ideas; some game features/certain statistics may be publicly inferable.
2. Proprietary combinations of mathematical logic, calculations, weights, confidential probabilities and internal spreadsheets.
3. Separate copyright/trade dress claims about artwork and visual presentation (the earlier Jewel of the Dragon looked similar allegation), not automatically identical to trade-secret game-math claims.

**Nuance that makes this research stronger than a generic copycat video:** In ECF125 the preliminary judge found core mathematical logic/weight combinations protected and a strong likelihood of misappropriation, **but accepted L&W arguments that certain SCAT2 probability values could be derived from visible features or target hit rates**. Do not state every matching probability was automatically secret. [S001 pp7–9]

## 4. The evidence chain — core reveal
**Court-described 2022 exchange:** In September 2022, Emma Charles shared a worksheet containing Dragon Link base-game information with a L&W colleague during a reverse-engineering training exercise. The Court noted its strikingly similar layout to an Aristocrat-era spreadsheet, including odd border shapes and same gray fills in empty cells. It also cited an email's confidential detail and a separate Teams exchange concerning a Grand Jackpot. These are **facts as assessed in an injunction ruling**, not sworn universal proof of every trade-secret charge at a completed trial. [S001 pp11–12]

**Dragon Train Game 5.xlsx** and earlier Excel material: In August 2024 Aristocrat sought to submit additional files from the L&W development context. The judge compared Dragon Train materials with Dragon Link spreadsheets Emma Charles had worked on when at Aristocrat in 2013. Matching prize assignment approaches, an identical Major Jackpot probability and uncommon non-functional spreadsheet characteristics mattered, not merely that both games showed dragons. [S001 pp12–15; S010 procedural supplement]

**The 2013 date:** A L&W-produced development spreadsheet (sometimes described as “Inception Conception”) bore a November 12, 2013 file-creation date that matched an earlier Dragon Link workbook; court discussed that the date predates Charles' 2021 arrival at L&W, adding provenance concern. Narrate as “the preliminary judge noted spreadsheet metadata that pointed back to 2013,” not as infallible forensics nor proof all current production source code was stolen. The underlying proprietary spreadsheet may be redacted/sealed; don't fabricate one and present it as authentic. [S001 pp13–14]

**Corporate slide / design goal:** The court cites an internal L&W PowerPoint describing an aim to preserve important elements from Dragon Link while adding a new experience, and a workbook note regarding a competitive sales pitch. Exact slide words require genuine source excerpt; short attributed paraphrase is safer. [S001 pp13–14]

**Defense considered:** L&W argued independent developers helped create its math, final math differed, game included additional features, and some coincidences reflected Charles' customary spreadsheet organization. Court considered those defenses and, on central issues, viewed Aristocrat as very likely to prevail at preliminary stage. Do not present the defenses as never raised; do not call interim likelihood a final liability award. [S001 pp14–16]

## 6. Exact money separation and risk audit
**$127,500,000 USD:** real negotiated settlement paid to Aristocrat, signed into US dismissal stipulation. Not a criminal penalty, not jury-measured damages, not necessarily the complete cost of litigation/removing units.

**Approx AUD $190m:** joint public approximate conversion, NOT separate consideration paid in addition to $127.5m. Don't sum.

**Approx $128m 2025 GAAP settlement expense:** L&W's 2025 full-year/Q4 results published 2026-02-24 report around $128m settlement charge and say Q4 net loss was affected. **Accounting recognition timing (2025) ≠ cash payment timing (2026).** These numbers are rounded/same settlement, not two separate $128m charges to add together. [S009]

**Less than 5% of $1.4 billion:** in Sept2024 L&W estimated Dragon Train pre-ruling 2025 AEBITDA would be less than 5% of management's targeted consolidated $1.4bn. **A non-GAAP forward projection**, not measured sales/cash impact, and not grounds for claiming the case cost an extra calculated $70m. [S004]

**$1m bond:** preliminary injunction security posted by Aristocrat and returned under Feb2026 order. Not money paid as part of L&W's $127.5m settlement. [S007]

**Sales/installations lost:** The company accepted cease-commercialization and best efforts at removal and had prior US compliance requirements, but there is NO verified complete worldwide count or audited loss on the present primary record. Do not claim a specific 1,000-unit/10,000-unit or total lost-profit number unless independently sourced and dated.

**Other games and potential residual costs:** the joint settlement reserves confidential procedures and arbitration regarding Aristocrat math in certain other hold-and-spin titles. No public “clean bill for entire catalog” or proven “every game copied.” [S006,S007]

## 7. What each side says and what the court found
**Aristocrat's pleaded theory:** years of expensive math R&D, confidential weighted game combinations, former staff knowledge and later rival game similarity; legal claims included trade-secret, copyright, trade dress, and deceptive practices. The side-by-side math evidence supported an injunction.

**L&W original 2024 reply:** denied the allegations and disagreed with the September injunction; said it would appeal, pointed to independently created game components, changed math, claimed some probabilities were observable/replicable, and maintained earnings outlook. The court considered these rather than simply ignoring them. [S001,S004]

**2024 judge's actual provisional result:** for injunction purposes the court regarded key Aristocrat math as trade secrets and found a high likelihood of improper access/use in Dragon Train. Important explicit nuance: some probabilistic similarities were NOT shown to be secret; the motion involved protected *combinations*, not merely all visible features. [S001]

**2026 agreement:** L&W acknowledged *certain* Aristocrat math info used with both families and agreed to permanent product commercialization restrictions, information deletion and compensation; its CEO offered former-employee explanation. The matter did not proceed to a full jury damages verdict. Temporary judicial restrictions were VACATED with case dismissal, yet contractual restrictions remain. [S006,S007]

**Australia:** Unlike the Nevada court, Australian court declined interlocutory relief in Feb2025, before settlement; L&W's public SEC later reports dismissal of Aristocrat's claims against specified L&W defendants Mar11 2026. Distinguish country, case number, and parties. [S008]
