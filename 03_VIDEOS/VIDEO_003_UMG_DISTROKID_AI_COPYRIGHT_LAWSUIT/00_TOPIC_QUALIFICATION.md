## Why it qualifies
UMG Recordings, Capitol Records and Capitol CMG filed the federal case on September 15, 2026. It combines recognizable music-industry entities, creator-economy/AI adjacency, concrete copyright allegations, a current docket and a hidden distribution mechanism.

The stronger angle is **not** "UMG sues AI music." The complaint expressly says the lawsuit is not about AI-generated music when clearly disclosed as such. The WHAT IT COST mechanism is the distributor as the gatekeeper/pipe between uploader and streaming service.
