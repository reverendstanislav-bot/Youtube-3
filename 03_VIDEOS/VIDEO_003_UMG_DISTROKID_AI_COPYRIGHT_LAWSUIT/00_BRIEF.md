## Working title
**Why Universal Music Just Sued DistroKid**

## Subject / entities
UMG Recordings, Capitol Records, Capitol CMG, DistroKid, Kid Distro Holdings and DK Holdco.

## Pillar
COPYRIGHT_AI

## Core viewer promise
Explain why Universal Music labels sued DistroKid, what UMG actually alleges, what DistroKid disputes, and why the real mechanism is the distributor sitting between uploader and streaming platform.

## Hidden problem / mechanism
Uploader → distributor → streaming service → rights-management conflict / notice → continued or stopped distribution. The case raises allegations about infringement, metadata/ISRC, mass-generated content and distributor conduct after notice.

## Money / stakes
There is **no fixed total damages figure pleaded as the value of the case**. UMG seeks injunctions plus statutory damages up to the applicable maximum per qualifying work where its allegations are proven, or alternatively actual damages and profits.

## Conflict
UMG alleges deceptive trade practices and copyright infringement. DistroKid publicly says it strongly disagrees, takes copyright/fraud prevention seriously, and will defend its practices.

## Consequence
Potential implications include upload verification, repeat-infringer handling, post-notice distribution, platform trust and the economics of high-volume distribution. These are potential consequences, not court findings.

## Why now
