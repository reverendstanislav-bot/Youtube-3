# VIDEO 002 — Stage 01B Visual Rights Notes

Status: **LOCKED**
Date: 2026-09-25

## Adobe
Adobe's downloadable executive-media page states that supplied leader images are for news-media articles/news reports and official-role events, with no endorsement implication.

Ordinary Adobe blog imagery is not assumed open-license. Images carrying separate photographer/agency credits remain HOLD_RIGHTS unless separately cleared.

## Figma
Figma product screenshots, event photos and blog visuals are authentic company context but are not treated as freely licensed stock. They remain YES_EDITORIAL only for direct documentary reporting about Figma, the product, the deal or the IPO.

## CMA / GOV.UK
Crown-copyright government material generally follows the Open Government Licence v3.0. Final asset selection must still check item-level exclusions and third-party credits.

## European Commission
Commission-owned website/document content defaults to CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise indicated. Third-party works, identifiable-person rights and industrial-property material can require separate clearance.

## DOJ
DOJ states that website information is public domain unless otherwise indicated. DOJ seals/logos remain protected; third-party credited images are not automatically public domain.

## SEC
EDGAR-hosted filings/contracts are treated as authentic documentary evidence. Use targeted excerpts with provenance and context; do not assume every embedded company-created element is independently open licensed.

## Stage 16
Every source actually surviving into the final cut gets a final rights/provenance refresh.
