# VIDEO 002 — Stage 01 Primary Source Pack

Status: **LOCKED / VERIFIED**
Research date: **2026-09-25**

This is the canonical Stage 01 primary-source map for the Adobe/Figma film. It is an evidence/research pack, not a visual-rights clearance pack.

## SEC / contract spine

### S003 — Definitive Merger Agreement
Controlling deal contract.
Key use:
- Section 8.2 $1B termination-fee mechanics;
- express language that the fee is **not a penalty** but liquidated damages;
- closing conditions and regulatory-efforts/remedy provisions.

### S004 — 424B3 Consent Solicitation / Prospectus
Best narrative source for how the deal was negotiated.
Key use:
- April–September 2022 negotiation chronology;
- June 19 $20B proposal initially without termination fee;
- Figma July 5 antitrust-linked reverse-fee proposal;
- Adobe July 20 agreement to a $1B reverse termination fee;
- approximately 50/50 cash/stock structure.

### S014 / S015 — 8-K + Mutual Termination Agreement
Best termination-mechanism sources.
Key use:
- Dec. 17, 2023 mutual termination;
- $1B cash due within three business days;
- liquidated-damages / sole-remedy structure;
- no regulator imposed the fee.

### S016 / S017 — Adobe annual filings
Best Adobe-side proof of actual payment/accounting.
Key use:
- Dec. 20, 2023 $1B cash payment;
- cash on hand;
- FY2024 operating-expense treatment.

## UK CMA spine

### S006 — canonical case page
Use for procedural chronology:
- May 3 inquiry opened;
- June 30 Phase 1 concern;
- July 13 Phase 2 referral;
- Nov. 28 provisional findings;
- Dec. 18 abandonment / cancellation;
- case closed as Phase 2 cancellation.

### S007 / S008 — provisional findings + possible remedies
Use only with **provisional** status.
CMA concerns:
- product design;
- vector editing;
- raster editing;
- potential effects on choice/innovation.
Possible remedies included prohibition or structural divestiture.

### S009 / S010 — parties' responses
Use for balance and counter-position.
Adobe/Figma argued:
- products/capabilities were complementary;
- transaction was pro-competitive;
- CMA remedies direction effectively pointed to prohibition or divestiture of Figma Design.

These are party positions, not independent findings.

## European Commission spine

### S011 — DG COMP Competition Merger Brief, M.11033
Official post-case source.
Records:
- notification: June 30, 2023;
- Phase II opening: Aug. 7;
- Statement of Objections: Nov. 17;
- abandonment: Dec. 18;
- **investigation ended without a formal Commission decision**.

Critical lock:
A Statement of Objections is preliminary. Do not say the EC formally prohibited the deal.

## U.S. DOJ spine

### S012 — DOJ Antitrust statement
Confirms DOJ Antitrust Division investigated the transaction and welcomed abandonment.

Critical lock:
Stage 01 located no public DOJ complaint/court judgment blocking the merger. Do not say DOJ sued Adobe to stop the deal unless a later primary filing is found.

## Figma post-deal spine

### S018 — Figma S-1
Confirms from Figma's side:
- mutual termination based on no clear path to required regulatory approvals;
- Dec. 20 receipt of $1B;
- abandoned-merger financial history.

### S019 — Figma 2025 10-K
Current post-deal anchor:
- Figma remained independent;
- $1B receipt remains in audited public-company record;
- IPO completed Aug. 1, 2025 at $33/share.

Critical lock:
The IPO is a later outcome, not evidence that the $1B fee caused Figma's IPO, IPO price, or subsequent valuation.

## Documentary-safe core chain

**$20B proposed acquisition → negotiated $1B antitrust-linked reverse termination fee → escalating UK/EU/US regulatory scrutiny → UK provisional structural-remedy risk + EU Statement of Objections → parties conclude no clear approval path → mutual termination → Adobe pays Figma $1B → Figma remains independent and later goes public.**

## Hard wording locks
1. $1B = contractual termination fee / liquidated damages.
2. $1B ≠ government fine.
3. $1B ≠ antitrust penalty.
4. $1B ≠ court award.
5. ~$20B = proposed consideration, not money Adobe paid.
6. CMA findings = provisional.
7. EC Statement of Objections = preliminary.
8. Neither CMA nor EC issued a final prohibition decision because the deal was abandoned.
9. DOJ investigated; do not invent a DOJ lawsuit.
10. Mutual termination is the formal transaction-ending mechanism.
