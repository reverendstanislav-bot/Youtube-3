## SH03 — What a Reverse Termination Fee Actually Is

Target duration:
40–55 sec

Long-form location:
Beat 3.

Hook:
**A reverse termination fee is basically a price the buyer agrees to pay if specified closing failures kill the deal.**

Standalone context:
Adobe/Figma merger agreement included a $1B fee tied to defined antitrust/closing circumstances.

Core mechanism:
contractual risk allocation.

Required legal caveat:
The agreement explicitly says the fee is **not a penalty** but liquidated damages.

Payoff:
So when the deal later died, Adobe did not get a regulator's invoice — the contract generated the payment obligation.

Visual mechanism:
contract clause excerpt → flow arrow → $1B.

Claim refs:
C005, C019.
