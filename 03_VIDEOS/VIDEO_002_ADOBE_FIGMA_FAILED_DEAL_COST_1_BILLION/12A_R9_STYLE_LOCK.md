# VIDEO 002 — R9 Unified 110 Final-Frame Style Lock

Status: **CANONICAL / OWNER-DIRECTED**
Date: 2026-09-27

## Production rule
**110 / 110 beats = 110 final-frame generation targets F001–F110.** The old 85 generated + 11 document + 6 UI + 8 GFX split is historical only.

No backplates. No placeholders. No “text later”. No editor-only beat. Each output is a finished 16:9 frame.

## Approved visual language
Owner-approved **NOT A FINE / THE RISK BECAME CASH** evidence-editorial language:
- deep charcoal-black textured environment;
- warm aged ivory paper/matte surfaces;
- distressed condensed ivory headline;
- one short deep-red headline underline;
- restrained deep-red bracket/tab/thread when meaningful;
- restrained warm-yellow translucent highlight only over authentic source text;
- low-key directional warm key light with subtle cool shadow falloff;
- fine film grain and modest wear;
- premium investigative business-documentary still;
- information first, decoration second.

## Source-first rule
Every F001–F110 prompt is repository-source-bound.
- Documents remain authentic document pixels.
- UI remains authentic UI pixels.
- Photos retain real faces/people/architecture/event composition.
- Logos/identifiers are not redrawn.
- Generation may frame, mount, light, crop, perspective-place or physically integrate a source, but may not replace it with a lookalike.
- Source failure = REJECT.

## Headline
Exactly one locked headline per frame: exact spelling/punctuation, distressed condensed ivory capitals, one or two lines where practical, one short deep-red underline, no secondary generated prose.

## Information density
One dominant idea; one dominant source/comparison; at most two non-source props; source text/UI must matter; empty charcoal space is preferable to filler.

## Families
- DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE — source fills about 55–70%; one bracket/highlight maximum.
- LAYERED_DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE — primary sharp, secondary offset/softer; no paper clutter.
- UI_EVIDENCE — authentic UI large/readable; native controls/colors preserved.
- UI_COMPARISON — asymmetric two-source hierarchy, not generic split-screen.
- ARCHIVAL_PHOTO / DUAL_ARCHIVAL_PHOTO — photos large and identifiable; faces unchanged.
- MIXED_EVIDENCE — one source clearly primary.
- INFO_GRAPHIC_FRAME — exact verified facts become a cinematic information composition grounded by attached sources; never PowerPoint.
- EDITORIAL_METAPHOR — metaphor carries the idea while factual source remains visible.

## Subtitle-safe
Bottom **15%** stays calm: no headline, face, key evidence, tiny UI, crucial date/number, or black caption box.

## Hard negatives
No fake legal body text, fake UI, invented logos, source lookalikes, extra people, warped faces, collage/contact sheets, giant icons/arrows/circles, generic random-paper desk filler, orange collage blocks, neon/glossy ad look, courtroom/gavel/Lady Justice clichés, unrelated flags/Capitol, piles of cash unless the beat literally requires them, or fake $21B arithmetic.

## QC
Only **PASS** or **REJECT**. No HOLD, no EDIT_FIX, no “verify later”.

PASS requires correct beat meaning, exact headline, all required sources present and source-faithful, R9 style intact, no legal/factual overstatement, bottom 15% subtitle-safe. Otherwise REJECT.
