## Slate rules

Before the ten-topic slate is locked, check:
- audience coherence across all ten;
- enough mix across lawsuits, contracts, ownership, creator/IP and fraud/business collapse;
- no cluster dominated by one company or one legal mechanism;
- at least several recognizable entities;
- a mix of timely and evergreen stories;
- strong visual potential;
- sourceability;
- no title dependent on an allegation being treated as fact;
- no obvious direct-repeat angle already used by a major competitor unless we have a better mechanism/source/story;
- enough adjacency for end-screen and playlist linking.

## Sequencing rules

The first ten are not ranked only by "best topic."

Sequence should consider:
- what the previous video teaches the audience to expect;
- visual variety;
- topical freshness;
- brand coherence;
- production complexity;
- legal/source risk;
- whether one upload creates a natural bridge to the next.

## Lock condition

Status changes to **LOCKED v1** only after the owner explicitly approves the ten-topic slate.

After lock:
1. Topic #1 enters full qualification/research.
2. Script #1 is written and locked.
3. Only after Script #1 lock do we select and lock the narrator/voice.
4. Voice selection must fit the actual script's pacing, sentence length, tension, humor level and documentary tone.

The voice is downstream of the script, not the other way around.
