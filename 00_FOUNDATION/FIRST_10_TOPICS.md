- trade secrets;

This prevents the channel from depending only on breaking litigation.

## Sequence logic

The planned sequence deliberately alternates:
- active vs resolved;
- consumer brand vs creator/platform;
- lawsuit vs contract vs trade secret;
- allegation-heavy vs outcome-heavy stories.

This reduces repetition and teaches the audience that WHAT IT COST is about **business consequences**, not one legal category.

## Freshness rule

Before activating any CURRENT / BREAKING topic:
- refresh current docket/status;
- re-check direct YouTube competition;
- re-check whether a settlement/ruling/new complaint materially changes the angle.

If freshness requires moving a locked topic earlier/later, sequence may change.

Changing the actual ten-topic membership requires owner approval.
