# Source Hierarchy

## Tier 1 — Primary / controlling evidence
Prefer whenever material and available:
- court complaints, answers, motions, orders, judgments, verdict forms and docket entries;
- statutes, regulations and official agency records;
- SEC/company filings;
- official company/party statements;
- contracts or exhibits authenticated in the record;
- public records and official transcripts.

## Tier 2 — High-quality original journalism
Major outlets or specialist reporters doing original reporting, especially when primary documents are unavailable or need context.

## Tier 3 — Specialist / trade reporting
Useful for industry context, chronology and discovery; corroborate material disputed claims.

## Tier 4 — General secondary context
Background only unless independently verified.

## Never sufficient alone for a material disputed claim
- AI-generated summaries;
- search snippets;
- aggregators;
- unsourced blogs;
- reposts;
- anonymous social claims;
- Reddit or forums.

## Rule
A source may prove that a person **said/alleged/claimed** something without proving the underlying assertion is true.
