# Editing Canon — WHAT IT COST

Status: OWNER_LOCKED v1
Approved: 2026-10-09
Scope: channel-wide editing behavior. Per-episode reference files, versions, hashes and QA evidence belong in that episode package.

## 1. Work from the locked picture
The approved frames, source documents and footage are the film. Develop them through framing, layering, timing and exact local emphasis. Do not restart an approved sequence, replace its visual language, fabricate evidence or generate new scenes during an edit revision without explicit owner instruction. Existing picture, narration and approved treatments are preserved outside the requested change. Montage and render are executed in DaVinci Resolve; FFmpeg may perform technical QC/extraction, not replace the edit with a slideshow. Visible editorial text and narration remain English.

## 2. Transitions follow the story
Each boundary needs a reason: continuation, new claim, enumeration, contrast, source/response, qualification or chapter. Prefer an unobtrusive cut when it communicates that relationship. Use a local paper/evidence handoff only when the source object motivates it. No cycling left/right/up/down wipes, universal cross-dissolves, random zooms, constant camera movement or shake. Variety comes from story, scale, duration, stillness and attention rather than an effects catalog.

## 3. Separate the events in a compound handoff
Do not move a card, replace the background and introduce a large heading in one instant. Stagger these events according to the beat: an insert may begin arriving while the outgoing heading fades; the background handoff stays beneath the protected foreground; the incoming heading becomes readable without competing large headings; a reading hold precedes the next accent. This is a relationship between layers, not a rigid serial template. Whole title groups fade without chopped letters or overlapping large headings. Preserve source-derived texture behind cleared text; avoid black masking bands and visible seams. A card starts with no abrupt visible-area pop and lands without a position/size jump. Opaque card coverage protects foreground text while the underlying page changes. A brief card entrance fade is permitted if text remains readable. Timing depends on the spoken beat, not a universal frame-count recipe.

## 4. Restrained motion with a named target
Every zoom has a specific document line, number, company object or mechanism as its destination. Deliberate eased motion starts and stops continuously; movement ends before the viewer must read. Keep a heading fixed while evidence moves when that preserves hierarchy. Avoid edge clipping, unstable interpolation and simultaneous unrelated motions. A calm frame is a valid authored choice.

## 5. Exact document accents
Map each highlight to source-native words and a narration cue. Track the actual line position, tilt and thickness; never highlight blank space or a neighboring line. Prefer a controlled left-to-right marker reveal aligned to that line, followed by enough hold to read. Preserve black glyph legibility; use local multiply treatment where appropriate. Underlines stay below the intended text, follow page angle and have a deliberate endpoint. Distinguish a fitted underline from a longer editorial rule. Not every document needs a marker. Semantic highlights are permitted in the approved subdued warm-yellow document-marker treatment; this does not introduce a new bright brand palette. Signal Red remains selective.

## 6. Insert choreography
Use an existing card/document crop to clarify evidence, response or comparison. Keep its relationship to the preceding shot intelligible. Reduce travel before increasing spectacle. Let the insert settle, give its title time to read, then apply a selective underline/accent timed to speech. Never add a new scene merely to make the edit look busy.

## 7. Preserve and verify
Keep the owner-approved version as an untouched reference; revisions use a separate timeline and identifiable output. Record the reference path/hash in the episode. Verify representative processed frames before scaling a treatment; inspect rendered boundary sequences and exact-line crops; obtain independent critique when available. Review the complete draft in real time with audio before release approval. Verify the actual output duration, frame count/rate, resolution, decode, black intervals and audio continuity. Contact sheets or a successful render cannot certify real-time smoothness. Owner approval of style establishes the creative baseline, not publication or full-film release approval.

## 8. Revision scope
Revise the specific failed behavior while preserving the successful style. Do not restart from zero after approval. Do not propagate an exact transition, zoom or highlight to every beat. Continue within existing user authorization. Additional scope, including unapproved full-film production, paid generation or publication, requires the applicable authorization; do not ask again for work already authorized.
