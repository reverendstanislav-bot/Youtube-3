# Positioning

## Core
**Business Stories Where Money & Law Collide**

## Viewer-facing promise
We uncover the hidden lawsuits, contracts, ownership fights and scandals behind companies, creators and products people already know.

## What the viewer comes for
- a surprising business story;
- a conflict with escalating stakes;
- a hidden clause, filing, ownership issue or legal mechanism;
- the money behind the fight;
- what ultimately changed because of it.

## What the channel is not
- generic legal education;
- law-school doctrine;
- daily court news;
- random crime coverage;
- celebrity gossip with weak business stakes;
- legal commentary without a narrative engine.

## Preferred packaging mechanics
- Just Got Caught
- It Backfired
- One Mistake Cost Millions
- One Deal Destroyed...
- The Real Contract
- Who Actually Owns...
- The Hidden Problem
- Founder Lost His Own Company
- Billion-Dollar Fight Over Something Simple
- Company vs Creator
- Company vs Company
- Tiny Legal Detail → Huge Consequence
