# Content Pillars

## P1 — Corporate Scandals & Business Collapse
Businesses whose hidden decisions, incentives, contracts or disputes materially changed the company.

## P2 — Business Lawsuits / Legal Wars
Two sides with understandable stakes, a concrete dispute, a legal mechanism and consequences beyond the courtroom.

## P3 — Founder / Partnership / Contract Disasters
Ownership, equity, partnership, licensing, employment or contract decisions that later became existential.

## P4 — Copyright / Creator / IP
Creator, platform, label, AI, copyright, trademark, licensing and ownership fights with strong business stakes.

## P5 — Business Fraud
Only when evidence quality is strong and wording precisely distinguishes allegations, charges, findings, admissions and convictions.

## Qualification principle
A pillar is not a topic. A topic must still have recognizability/novelty, conflict, money stakes, a hidden mechanism, sourceability, visual potential and a meaningful consequence.
