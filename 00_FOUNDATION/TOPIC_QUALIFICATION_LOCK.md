# Topic Qualification Lock

A topic does not enter production merely because it is legally interesting.

## Required channel fit
A strong topic should combine:
- recognizable company / creator / product OR an extraordinary story;
- meaningful money / ownership / business stakes;
- clear conflict;
- a hidden problem or mechanism;
- lawsuit / contract / IP / ownership / fraud / regulation as the mechanism;
- a concrete consequence;
- enough reliable sources to tell the story safely and precisely.

## Hard fails
NO-GO regardless of score when:
- the topic is mainly doctrine/explanation rather than story;
- primary/current evidence for the core dispute is unavailable and secondary reporting is too weak;
- the strongest hook depends on stating an allegation as fact;
- the story has no clear consequence or payoff;
- the topic is already so saturated that we have no meaningful new angle;
- the legal/reputational risk cannot be reduced to a manageable level through sourcing and wording.

## Decision labels
- GO — qualifies for package creation / production.
- HOLD — promising but blocked by sourcing, freshness, packaging or differentiation.
- NO-GO — does not fit the channel or fails a hard gate.
