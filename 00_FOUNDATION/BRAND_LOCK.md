## Brand idea
Every episode reveals what a business decision, deal, conflict, hidden term or legal fight ultimately cost.

The name is intentionally broader than law. It must support:
- corporate scandals;
- business lawsuits;
- founder / partnership conflicts;
- contracts and ownership fights;
- copyright / creator disputes;
- fraud and business failures.

## Tagline
**Where business decisions get expensive.**

## Public promise
**The hidden lawsuits, deals and decisions behind companies, creators and products people already know.**

## Public About copy
WHAT IT COST tells the stories behind companies, creators and products people already know — following the money, contracts, lawsuits and decisions that changed what happened next.

Each episode digs into the documents, incentives and conflicts behind the headline, separating allegations from established facts and explaining the legal or business mechanism without turning the story into a law lecture.

Documentary storytelling. Not legal advice.
