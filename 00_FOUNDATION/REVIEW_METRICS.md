# Review Metrics

All reviews produce PASS / WARN / FAIL plus exact issues.

## Research readiness
Must have:
- central story question;
- material chronology;
- primary-source coverage for core legal events where practical;
- money/stakes support;
- opposing positions;
- unresolved uncertainties identified;
- competitor treatment/gap;
- visual evidence opportunities.

## Fact/legal hard metrics
Each is 0–5; all hard metrics must be >=4 and no BLOCKER:
1. claim-to-source traceability;
2. allegation/status precision;
3. procedural chronology;
4. quote/context accuracy;
5. current-status freshness;
6. numerical accuracy;
7. party-position fairness;
8. primary-source sufficiency.

## Script performance
Weighted 100:
- Hook 12
- First 30 seconds 10
- Title/thumbnail promise alignment 10
- Conflict escalation 10
- Curiosity/reveal architecture 10
- Payoff frequency 8
- Information density 8
- Character clarity 6
- Novelty/differentiation 8
- Visualizability 8
- Ending/consequence 6
- Next-view potential 4

PASS >=82 with Hook, Promise, Conflict, Accuracy >=4/5 and no BLOCKER.
WARN 75–81 with no hard failure.
FAIL <75, any BLOCKER, or a hard metric failure.

## Release
Final fact/legal refresh must PASS after edit and before publication.
