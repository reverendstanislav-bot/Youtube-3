# Channel Locks

## Direction
- English-language, USA-focused, faceless long-form documentary/storytelling channel.
- Core runtime target for early videos: approximately 15–22 minutes.
- Positioning: **Business Stories Where Money & Law Collide**.
- Promise: **The hidden lawsuits, contracts and scandals behind companies, creators and products people already know.**
- Law is the mechanism of the story, not the lecture topic.

## Story formula
`Company / Creator + Money + Conflict + Hidden Problem + Legal Mechanism + Consequence`

## Core pillars
1. Corporate Scandals & Business Collapse.
2. Business Lawsuits / Legal Wars.
3. Founder / Partnership / Contract Disasters.

Secondary:
4. Copyright / Creator / IP.
5. Business Fraud.

## Editorial locks
- Story first, doctrine second.
- No generic “lawyer explains the law” identity.
- No dry doctrine-first episodes.
- A recognizable entity/product or extraordinary story is strongly preferred.
- Big stakes alone are insufficient; the hidden mechanism and consequence must be clear.
- Allegation is never fact merely because it appears in a complaint.
- Current case status must be refreshed before final publication.
