# Storytelling

## Story formula
`Company / Creator + Money + Conflict + Hidden Problem + Legal Mechanism + Consequence`

Law is the mechanism of the story, not the topic.

## Story engine
1. Familiar surface — a company/product/creator people know.
2. Hidden problem.
3. Stakes — money, ownership, power, reputation, survival.
4. Conflict — sides with incompatible goals.
5. Mechanism — contract, lawsuit, IP, trade secret, fraud theory, arbitration, regulation.
6. Escalation — filings, discoveries, mistakes, reversals.
7. Reveal — the clause/document/action that reframes everything.
8. Consequence — what it ultimately cost.

The viewer leaves knowing: what looked normal → what was underneath → how money shaped behavior → what the mechanism did → what changed → what it cost.

## Packaging-first
The title + thumbnail concept is chosen at GO, before the script. The first 30 seconds must confirm that promise; the whole video must pay it off.

## Opening
No history lesson. Open on the contradiction, the extraordinary claim, the money figure, the recognizable object, or the decisive consequence. State procedural status early enough that suspense never rests on a false statement.

## Chronology
`events.csv` is canonical chronology. Narrative may reorder reveals but may never create false causality.

## Legal exposition
Explain only what unlocks the story, then return to people, money, decisions, consequences.

## Ending
Concrete consequence + unresolved implication. If the matter is still active, say so. No CTA before the final WHAT IT COST line.

## Rhythm
Change how the viewer processes information when the story changes function:
real place → evidence → explanation → consequence → person/status → movement → payoff.
Never three identical layouts in a row.

## Shorts (mandatory)
- 6–10 Shorts per video, written into the long-form script from v1.
- Each Short = **one continuous range** of the final narration, 25–60 s (≤75 s if a legal unit needs it), cut on sentence boundaries.
- Inside that range: standalone hook → enough context → one conflict/number/mechanism/reveal → payoff → any caveat needed.
- No reordered narration, no stitching distant lines, no separate voice-over.
- Each Short is fact/legal-reviewed in isolation: extraction must never turn allegation → fact, charge → conviction, settlement → judgment, attempt → completed act.

## Avoid
Saturated evergreen topics without a genuinely new angle (Theranos, WeWork, Pepsi jet, McDonald's hot coffee, Swift vs Braun). Doctrine-first topics. Daily court news. Celebrity gossip with weak business stakes.

## Title mechanics (structures, not permission to overstate)
Just Got Caught · It Backfired · One Mistake Cost Millions · One Deal Destroyed… · The Real Contract Behind… · Who Actually Owns… · The Hidden Problem With… · How a Founder Lost His Own Company · The Billion-Dollar Fight Over… · Company vs Creator · Company vs Company · One Tiny Clause Changed Everything.
