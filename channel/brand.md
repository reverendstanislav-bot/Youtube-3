# Brand — WHAT IT COST

Tagline: **Where business decisions get expensive.**
Promise: **The hidden lawsuits, deals and decisions behind companies, creators and products people already know.**
Positioning: **Business Stories Where Money & Law Collide.**

## Format
- English, US audience, faceless long-form documentary.
- Target runtime 15–22 min.
- 6–10 Shorts cut from every long-form (see `storytelling.md` → Shorts).
- **Audio = narration only. No music, no SFX, no room tone.** Owner decision after VIDEO_001; do not propose music/SFX unless the owner reopens it.

## Pillars
1. Corporate scandals & business collapse
2. Business lawsuits / legal wars
3. Founder / partnership / contract disasters
4. Copyright / creator / IP (secondary)
5. Business fraud (secondary; only with strong evidence and precise status wording)

## Tone
Curious, controlled, cinematic, precise. Skeptical, never tabloid. A premium business documentary that understands the legal mechanism — not a law lecture, not a law firm.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Black | `#0B0B0B` | base |
| Charcoal | `#1F1F1F` | base |
| Slate | `#3A3A3A` | base |
| Paper White | `#EDEDED` | text, documents |
| Signal Red | `#D32222` | one semantic accent per frame: key word, amount, date, status |
| Antique Gold | `#B69B68` | rare, money only; never competes with red |

No blue/green/purple/neon. Orange is retired.

## Type
- Display: **Bebas Neue** — headlines, amounts, thumbnail text.
- Support: **Inter** — dates, labels, sources, diagrams.
- Serif: **EB Garamond** — rare, one-line quotes only.
- Captions: **Montserrat ExtraBold**, 46 px @1080p, `#EDEDED`, black outline 3.2, active word `#D32222`, max ~53 chars per line, 2 lines max.
- Max two type families per thumbnail.

## Safe zones
- 16:9: bottom **20%** (Y 864–1080 @1080p) reserved for captions. No headline, number, face, evidence, source label or caveat there.
- 9:16: bottom **22%** (from Y ≈1498 @1920) reserved for captions + platform UI.

## Thumbnail grammar
- 1 dominant subject + 1 evidence/context element + 1 tension device + restrained red + negative space.
- Text 0–4 words (5 max), adds a second layer to the title — never repeats it.
- Readable at phone size: who/what → conflict/stakes → one open question.
- Master 3840×2160; upload derivative 1280×720.
- Forbidden words unless status supports them: GUILTY, FRAUD, STOLE, SCAM, CRIMINAL.
- No fake reactions of real people; no mugshot language unless verified and relevant.

## Never as brand marks
Gavels, scales, courthouse columns, handcuffs, dollar-sign monograms, stock candlesticks, legal seals.
